## 2. Currency, lineage, and rights

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-CUR-01"></a>
- Pokrifka et al. 2020 reanalyzes these heterogeneous mass-ratio records, corrects the ambient
  supersaturation calibration and several initial radii, and revises the fitted-kinetics
  interpretation. It is a same-data correction, not a second witness. Phase 8 must pair these raw
  files with the 2020 Table 1 conditions indexed in [`pokrifka-2020.md`](pokrifka-2020.md).

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-CUR-02"></a>
- Pokrifka, Moyle, and Harrington 2023, DOI
  [`10.1175/JAS-D-22-0077.1`](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-22-0077.1), uses an upgraded BEL
  chamber at -65 to -40 C and archives a separate, larger particle population. It is later
  apparatus-lineage evidence, not a replacement for these 2016 traces.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-CUR-03"></a>
- The later morphology analysis, DOI
  [`10.1175/JAS-D-23-0131.1`](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-23-0131.1), reports that BEL
  frozen-droplet products are likely polycrystalline. That qualification must accompany any
  single-prism or single-crystal interpretation of the 2016/2020 witness.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-CUR-04"></a>
- Official AMS, Penn State, and NSF records searched through 2026-08-10 exposed no corrigendum or
  replacement of DOI `10.1175/JAS-D-15-0234.1`. This is the result of the recorded source-currency
  search, not a claim to have exhausted every publication.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-RIGHTS-01"></a>
- The article is American Meteorological Society copyright and its official page uses a permissions
  workflow; no item-specific open license was identified. The Data Commons record has no access
  restriction, but its use terms grant a narrow translation/value-add permission for use on the
  user's computer hardware and request annual notification. That is not a broad redistribution
  license. Do not commit the ZIP, raw archive bodies, article figures, or substantial table content
  without a separate rights determination.

## 4. Source-reported protocol tags

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-PROT-01"></a>
- **Seed/nucleation:** individual small water droplets, approximately 10 micrometres in initial
  radius, were frozen heterogeneously with the bionucleant Snomax. The later official apparatus
  description identifies the mixture as 0.2 g L^-1 Snomax in HPLC water. Crystallographic
  orientation and grain count were not measured (`DataDescription`; XML Description/Abstract;
  Pokrifka et al. 2023, section 2).

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-PROT-02"></a>
- **Transport/geometry:** a charged particle was electrodynamically levitated, vertically by the
  lower-plate DC potential and laterally by four upper AC button electrodes. Growth was away from
  a support substrate. Later official apparatus-lineage text gives parallel copper plates separated
  by a 1.27 cm-tall, 10.2 cm-diameter ring, an approximately 8:1 diameter-to-gap ratio, and ice-fed
  plate surfaces (official 2016 abstract; Pokrifka et al. 2023, section 2).

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-PROT-03"></a>
- **Pressure:** ambient pressure was approximately 970 hPa. Neither the official abstract nor the
  local condition metadata supplies a pressure uncertainty (official 2016 abstract;
  `Conditions.txt`).

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-PROT-04"></a>
- **Temperature and original condition scope:** the article abstract reports -30 to -35.7 C and
  ambient ice supersaturation 2.5-28.6%. The archive supplemental text says -30 to -37 C. Per-run
  temperature, ambient supersaturation, and initial-radius values in `Conditions.txt` are historical
  values superseded by Pokrifka 2020 where a corrected row exists.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-PROT-05"></a>
- **Supersaturation semantics:** the archived value is chamber-calibrated ice supersaturation at the
  particle location. It is an ambient chamber condition, not surface supersaturation. The stated
  per-case uncertainty belongs to the calibration; covariance with temperature or initial radius is
  not reported (`Conditions.txt`; XML Supplemental_Information).

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-PROT-06"></a>
- **History:** the official archive describes temperature, pressure, and ambient supersaturation as
  constant within each trace. Each `.dat` file is one selected particle's longitudinal record, not
  cross-time ensemble snapshots (XML Description/Abstract and Supplemental_Information).

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-PROT-07"></a>
- **Measurement:** initial liquid-droplet radius was measured through the He-Ne/Mie-scattering
  response. During growth, automatic lower-plate-voltage changes maintained levitation; normalized
  voltage directly supplied `m/m0`. No later particle dimensions, habit, facet orientation, or
  grain structure were directly measured (official apparatus lineage; XML Description/Abstract).

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-PROT-08"></a>
- **Population semantics:** the archive is 17 individual selected traces, not a population
  frequency or unbiased habit survey. File letters distinguish multiple experiments on a date
  (`DataDescription`; XML Supplemental_Information).

## 5. Archive column semantics and measured scan facts

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-DATA-01"></a>
- Each `.dat` row has six numeric columns: program voltage-modification number, levitation voltage,
  elapsed seconds, image position in pixels, normalized mass `m/m0`, and a column labeled
  `mass(*10^9)` (`DataDescription`; `README-Data`; XML Supplemental_Information).

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-DATA-02"></a>
- The base unit for the sixth column is absent from all three data-description members. It is not an
  eligible observable. Phase 8 may use only elapsed time and the directly reported fifth-column
  `m/m0`, with corrected conditions joined from Pokrifka 2020.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-DATA-03"></a>
- A complete finite-value/shape scan found all 17 `.dat` members parse as six-column numeric data
  with nondecreasing timestamps. Raw `m/m0` is not monotone: per-file adjacent decreases range from
  zero to 57. Monotonic regression would erase observed variation and is not source-authorized.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-DATA-04"></a>
- Measured time domains in seconds are: `712a` 0.003-473.558; `712k` 0-1050; `716a` 0-688.054;
  `716d` 0-611.034; `716k` 0-1164.388; `724b` 0-3610.676; `724c` 0-581.391; `725c`
  0-1036.723; `725e` 0-920.709; `731a` 0-1725.537; `731b` 0-1224.098; `802d`
  0-1311.368; `805a` 0-449.591; `805b` 0-463.613; `805h` 0.360-323.809; `805l`
  0-311.332; and `625` 0-1361.962 (named `.dat` members, complete row scan).

## 6. Direct observations versus interpretations

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-OBS-01"></a>
- **Direct observation:** the longitudinal target is the measured mass-ratio series `m/m0(t)` for
  each eligible archived particle. Initial radius and chamber conditions are inputs with stated
  source uncertainty; they are not target outputs (named `.dat` member plus corrected Pokrifka 2020
  Table 1 row).

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-OBS-02"></a>
- **Source-level qualitative observation:** the official abstract reports nonlinear mass growth and
  dependence on initial radius and ambient ice supersaturation. The raw archive makes the mass-ratio
  part independently recomputable; causal attribution still depends on the condition calibration.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-INT-01"></a>
- **Model interpretation, not target:** the 2016 abstract reports effective shape factors around
  0.5 and fitted attachment coefficient `alphaHK` from 0.003 to 0.02. Neither quantity is directly
  measured: both depend on the then-current chamber calibration, capacitance/shape assumptions, and
  a theoretical fit. Pokrifka 2020 supersedes this fitted range for the same witness.

## 7. Anomalies and exclusions

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-LIM-01"></a>
- `heticegrowth_712k.dat` contains 10,445 rows but only 1,595 unique timestamps: 8,850 adjacent
  repeated timestamps, 1,193 duplicate-time groups, and maximum multiplicity 22. Columns 1, 2, and
  4 are zero placeholders. Any target extraction must register duplicate-time handling.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-LIM-02"></a>
- `heticegrowth_716d.dat` has an initial sixth-column absolute-mass value inconsistent with the
  corrected 5.8 +/- 0.4 micrometre radius. Because that column's unit is also unspecified, retain
  direct `m/m0` and reject the sixth column rather than selecting whichever implied radius fits.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-LIM-03"></a>
- `heticegrowth_805l.dat` is paired by Pokrifka 2020 with corrected radius 8.9 +/- 0.7 micrometres;
  the stale archive value is 8.29 micrometres with asymmetric bounds. Use the corrected row.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-LIM-04"></a>
- `heticegrowth_625.dat` does not reconcile with the corrected heterogeneous table: its archived
  condition is outside the stated heterogeneous temperature domain, while the same 21.55
  micrometre radius appears in Pokrifka 2020's homogeneous family at -43.4 C. Exclude the member
  pending provenance resolution.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-LIM-05"></a>
- Pokrifka 2020 prints a heterogeneous row at -31.5 +/- 0.1 C, 5.0 +/- 0.6% ambient ice
  supersaturation, and 10.69 +/- 0.17 micrometres without a corresponding archive member. Do not
  synthesize it, relabel `625`, or substitute a neighboring record.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-LIM-06"></a>
- The abstract's nominal 5-15 min duration does not cover `heticegrowth_724b.dat`, whose archived
  time reaches 3610.676 s. The raw time coordinate controls any extraction; the abstract range must
  not be used as a truncation rule.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-LIM-07"></a>
- No growth-time image or habit record accompanies the mass traces. Later BEL morphology work's
  likely-polycrystalline result prevents Phase 8 from labeling these records as observed
  single-crystal prisms.

## 9. Provisional Phase 8 implications - project interpretation

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-P8-01"></a>
- **Witness:** combine Harrison 2016 raw traces and Pokrifka 2020 corrected conditions/reanalysis as
  one Penn State BEL frozen-droplet witness. Counting the two papers separately would duplicate the
  observations.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-P8-02"></a>
- **Robustness:** direct `m/m0(t)` is a provisional Class-B, protocol-bound target candidate. Required
  tags are Snomax-frozen droplet, selected particle, approximately 970 hPa, electrodynamic
  levitation, static chamber, no substrate contact, constant corrected ambient condition, and
  unobserved/likely-polycrystalline morphology.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-P8-03"></a>
- **Inputs versus targets:** corrected temperature, chamber-calibrated ambient ice supersaturation,
  pressure, initial radius, and nucleation protocol are inputs. Direct mass ratio is the candidate
  target. Fitted `alphaHK`, effective shape factor, inferred dimensions, and assigned habit are not
  target observations.

<a id="P8X-HARRISON16-P8-04"></a>
- **Operator dependency:** duplicate coalescing, interpolation or resampling, error-envelope use,
  and any derivative/exponent calculation remain S5 work. This S0 index alone authorizes no score or
  validation claim.
