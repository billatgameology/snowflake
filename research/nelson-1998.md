## 2. Reading convention and complete protocol tags

`Fact (paraphrase)` means a source-stated fact restated without quotation. `Short quote` is exact
source language and counts toward the 24-word quotation total above. `Audit limit` and
`provisional implication` are project judgments, not statements by Nelson.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-SEED"></a> **Seed - fact (paraphrase):** a single ice crystal of
about 10 um outside diameter was formed at the tip of a glass capillary of about 5 um outside
diameter by freezing liquid water inside the capillary (printed p. 910 / PDF p. 1).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-MEDIUM"></a> **Medium and transport geometry - fact
(paraphrase):** the capillary was stationary in an isothermal chamber containing unstirred air and
water vapor; no airflow was intentionally imposed (printed pp. 910-911 / PDF pp. 1-2). This is a
supported, stationary crystal, not a free-falling or ventilated crystal.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-PRESSURE"></a> **Pressure - audit limit:** the paper identifies
air as the background gas but reports no chamber pressure. Phase 8 must preserve pressure as
`unreported`; it may not assume 1 atm or infer pressure from the diffusion fit.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-HUMIDITY-CONTROL"></a> **Humidity control - fact
(paraphrase):** a much-larger aqueous LiCl puddle acted as vapor source/sink; changing LiCl
concentration moved its equilibrium crossing with ice, and warming or cooling across that crossing
selected sublimation or growth (printed pp. 910-911 / PDF pp. 1-2; Fig. 1).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-TEMPERATURE-UNCERTAINTY"></a> **Temperature uncertainty -
fact (paraphrase):** immersion in a constant-temperature bath controlled temperature to within
0.03 C (printed p. 910 / PDF p. 1). The paper does not provide a fuller uncertainty budget.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-SIGMA-SEMANTICS"></a> **Supersaturation semantics - short
quote:** "The undersaturation is determined to within 0.03%." (printed p. 910 / PDF p. 1).
This index interprets the printed percent as an absolute percentage-point read uncertainty in
ice-relative undersaturation, not a relative percent of the undersaturation value.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-HISTORY"></a> **Growth history - fact (paraphrase):** a crystal
was first grown by cooling until it was about 100 um, then the chamber was warmed above the
LiCl-solution freezing point to drive sublimation; individual crystals frequently experienced
several growth/sublimation cycles (printed p. 911 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-ENSEMBLE"></a> **Ensemble semantics - short quote:**
"Approximately 100 crystals were grown and sublimated in this manner" (printed p. 911 / PDF
p. 2). This is an approximate experiment-wide count, not a per-condition sample count or a
published row-level dataset.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-IMPURITY-HISTORY"></a> **Impurity/history tag - fact
(paraphrase):** crystals were grown in unfiltered laboratory air for as long as several days and
often cycled repeatedly, so the authors considered accumulated impurity plausible (printed pp.
915-916 / PDF pp. 6-7).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-PROTOCOL-SUBSTRATE"></a> **Substrate interaction - fact (paraphrase):**
the source argues that the capillary did not appear to influence sublimation, while acknowledging
the supported geometry; the claimed advantage is minimal, not proven-zero, substrate influence
(printed p. 911 / PDF p. 2).

## 3. Page-cited outcome and numeric candidate extraction

<a id="P8X-NELSON-DOMAIN"></a> **Executed domain - fact (paraphrase):** sublimation was
monitored from -18 to -0.1 C at ice-relative undersaturations from 0.05% to 5% (printed p. 911 /
PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-ROUNDING"></a> **Population-level morphology - short quote:** "all
crystals evolved toward completely rounded shapes" (printed p. 911 / PDF p. 2). The statement
applies to the approximate 100-crystal experiment under the reported stationary-air protocol; it
does not establish all atmospheric sublimation behavior.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-RATE-DIFFUSION"></a> **Rate behavior - fact (paraphrase):** measured
sublimation rates were reported as accurately predicted by the diffusion equation when the surface
vapor density was set to equilibrium (printed p. 911 / PDF p. 2). Numerical residuals or a pooled
goodness-of-fit statistic are not printed.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-SHAPE-SPHEROID"></a> **Terminal shape - fact (paraphrase):** crystals
became nearly spheroidal, and height/width stayed approximately constant and close to the value at
the end of growth (printed p. 911 / PDF p. 2). The paper does not tabulate final aspect ratios.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-ROUNDING-DOMAIN"></a> **Domain robustness - fact (paraphrase):**
rounding was observed at every measurable undersaturation and temperature, including -18 C, the
coldest condition studied (printed p. 911 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-FIG2-CONDITIONS"></a> **Figure 2 candidate - fact (paraphrase):** a solid
crystal at -1.2 C and 1.0% undersaturation was imaged initially and after 1 h 14 min; plotted major
and minor semiaxes were compared with a spheroid diffusion calculation (printed p. 911 / PDF
p. 2). The calculation used sublimation coefficient 1, while the caption says any coefficient above
0.1 would produce nearly the same curve.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-FIG2-UNCERTAINTY"></a> **Figure 2 uncertainty - fact (paraphrase):** its
error bars, and those in the following plots, represent uncertainty in measured size and image
scale; the caption supplies no numeric bar-generation rule (printed p. 911 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-FIG3-CONDITIONS"></a> **Figure 3 candidate - fact (paraphrase):** a solid
crystal at -1.0 C and 1.3% undersaturation was imaged initially and after 15 min; major and minor
semiaxes versus time were compared with the Figure 2 model constants (printed p. 912 / PDF p. 3).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-FIG4-CONDITIONS"></a> **Figure 4 candidate - fact (paraphrase):** a crystal
with initially incomplete basal faces (hollows) was sublimated at -0.9 C and 1.4%
undersaturation; its major and minor semiaxes are plotted, and the dashed curve is explicitly a
hand fit (printed p. 912 / PDF p. 3).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-FIG5-HISTORY-A"></a> **Figure 5 history A - fact (paraphrase):** the
Figure 2 crystal's side outline is shown at the initial growth shape and after 50 and 66 min of
sublimation (printed p. 913 / PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-FIG5-HISTORY-B"></a> **Figure 5 history B - fact (paraphrase):** a front
view at -1.0 C and 1.3% undersaturation is shown initially and after 20 and 36 min; later shapes
were reported indistinguishable from the last shown (printed p. 913 / PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-NELSON-HOLLOW-POLY-ROUNDING"></a> **Non-solid-form qualification - fact
(paraphrase):** hollow and polycrystalline crystals also rounded, but took much longer to become
spheroidal (printed p. 917 / PDF p. 8). No time ratio, condition count, or uncertainty is supplied.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-FACET-LIFETIME"></a> **Facet-loss estimate - fact (paraphrase):** a model
calculation estimates complete facet loss after about 50% of total crystal lifetime, independent of
aspect ratio and undersaturation; the paper calls agreement rough and says the estimate was not
sufficiently tested (printed p. 917 / PDF p. 8). Treat this as a low-confidence model-derived
candidate, not a measured universal target.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-IMPURITY-NULL"></a> **Impurity control - fact (paraphrase):** the authors
could detect a 0.5% supersaturation change but observed no lowering of equilibrium vapor pressure
despite possible impurity accumulation (printed pp. 915-916 / PDF pp. 6-7). This is a bounded null
observation under their apparatus, not an impurity-free certification.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-MEAN-FREE-PATH"></a> **Theory-derived scale - fact (paraphrase):** the
paper estimates a vapor mean free path of about 0.2 um and a typical crystal radius near 100 um,
giving a denominator term near 500 and an argument that surface kinetics would affect the rate only
for a sublimation coefficient below roughly 1/500 (printed p. 913 / PDF p. 4). These are model
operands/inferences, not independent measured targets.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-STEP-SCALES"></a> **Theory-derived surface scales - fact (paraphrase):**
the argument combines an externally sourced surface-migration distance of order 10,000 angstrom
with a visually inferred typical step spacing below about 300 angstrom to infer near-unity surface
sublimation efficiency (printed pp. 912-914 / PDF pp. 3-5). None is a direct row-level measurement
from this experiment.

## 5. Conflicts, limits, and provisional Phase 8 implications

<a id="P8X-NELSON-CONFLICT-TABLE1"></a> **Internal presentation tension - audit limit:**
Results and Figure 5 document transient rounding before a later time-independent outline (printed
pp. 911-913 / PDF pp. 2-4), while Table 1 broadly summarizes whether shape changes with time
as no for sublimation (printed p. 917 / PDF p. 8). A target must name the transient interval and
observable rather than inherit the table shorthand.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-LIMIT-FREEFALL"></a> **Transfer limit - fact (paraphrase):** the authors
state that stationary-crystal results do not apply directly to large crystals in free fall (printed
p. 911 / PDF p. 2). Ventilation, pressure, size, and support geometry must therefore match before
cross-lab pooling.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-LIMIT-NO-RAW"></a> **Data limit - audit fact:** no machine-readable
per-crystal rows, condition-level sample counts, aspect-ratio table, or numeric fit residuals are
printed. Figures 2-5 are the only local quantitative/image trajectories and remain undigitized.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-IMPLICATION-TARGET"></a> **Provisional target role:** rounding, near-
spheroidal shape evolution, approximate aspect-ratio preservation, and size-time sublimation
trajectories are candidate negative-drive/history **targets**. The preparation size, LiCl control,
and imposed undersaturation are protocol inputs, not outcomes.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-IMPLICATION-INPUT"></a> **Provisional input/interpretation role:** the
sublimation-coefficient argument, step scales, 1/500 threshold, and 50%-of-lifetime estimate are
model-dependent interpretation or input-like quantities. They must not validate a model that
adopts them.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-IMPLICATION-CLASS"></a> **Provisional robustness:** this source alone is
at most Class B (one laboratory, one supported stationary-air method). A later independent
witness could promote only the specifically matched observable; same-author follow-ons are not
independent witnesses.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-IMPLICATION-MODEL-SCOPE"></a> **Provisional model-scope flag:** this is
sublimation after growth, often with repeated history. It is not an apples-to-apples comparator for
the present positive-growth-only solver unless Phase 9 adds a registered negative-drive and history
operator.

<a id="P8X-NELSON-IMPLICATION-SPLIT"></a> **Held-out status:** unassigned in S0. No target
from this paper may be called held out, frozen, or Class A until Phase 8 S3-S6 completes.
