## 2. Currency, witness lineage, and rights

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-CUR-01"></a>
- The heterogeneous subset is a reanalysis of the Harrison et al. 2016 Penn State BEL archive, not
  a new measurement campaign. Harrison 2016 plus Pokrifka 2020 therefore forms one observational
  witness: Harrison supplies raw `m/m0(t)` and Pokrifka supplies the corrected chamber conditions
  and revised interpretation (manuscript pp. 16-18 and Table 1, p. 37).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-CUR-02"></a>

  [`10.1175/JAS-D-20-0228.1`](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-20-0228.1), develops approximate
  lateral-growth models using this analysis lineage. It qualifies mechanism inference from fitted
  curves but does not replace the raw mass ratios.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-CUR-03"></a>

  [`10.1175/JAS-D-22-0077.1`](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-22-0077.1), reports a distinct,
  upgraded BEL dataset at -65 to -40 C. It is additional apparatus-lineage evidence, not a
  correction to these 2016 heterogeneous files.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-CUR-04"></a>
- The later morphology analysis, DOI
  [`10.1175/JAS-D-23-0131.1`](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-23-0131.1), reports that BEL
  frozen-droplet products are likely polycrystalline. Phase 8 must carry that qualification instead
  of promoting the manuscript's possible single-crystal explanation into an observation.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-RIGHTS-01"></a>

## 4. Source-reported protocol tags

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-01"></a>
- **Seed/nucleation:** the heterogeneous family reuses Snomax-frozen droplets from Harrison 2016;
  the other family contains 17 homogeneously frozen HPLC-water particles. This index keeps the
  families distinct and does not count them as independent laboratories (manuscript pp. 16, 18,
  22-23).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-02"></a>
- **Transport/geometry:** particles grew individually in the Button Electrode Levitation thermal-
  gradient diffusion chamber. The lower plate's DC voltage levitated the charged particle and four
  upper AC electrodes stabilized it laterally. The particle grew without a support substrate in a
  static chamber, not free fall (manuscript pp. 7-9).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-03"></a>
- **Pressure:** the manuscript's model examples and apparatus lineage use approximately 970 hPa;
  the paper does not print a pressure uncertainty for the experimental table (manuscript pp. 8,
  13; Figure 2, p. 42).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-04"></a>
- **Temperature/supersaturation domain:** the combined paper reports -44 to -30 C and chamber-
  calibrated ambient ice supersaturation approximately 3-29%. Corrected heterogeneous rows occupy
  -35.7 to -30.9 C and 4.0-28.6% (abstract p. 2; Table 1, p. 37).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-05"></a>
- **Supersaturation semantics:** `sigma_i` is ambient chamber ice supersaturation at the particle,
  inferred from the revised flat-plate chamber calibration. It is neither directly measured
  surface supersaturation nor the solver's automatically equivalent boundary value. Per-row
  uncertainty is printed in Table 1; pressure uncertainty and joint covariance are not reported
  (manuscript pp. 8, 13, 28-30, 37).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-06"></a>
- **History:** chamber conditions are treated as constant during each mass-growth trace. Figure 16
  is a separate low-temperature-difference calibration experiment and must not be substituted as
  the history for the Table 1 records (manuscript pp. 11-13, 28-30, 56).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-07"></a>
- **Measurement:** normalized levitation voltage provides direct mass ratio `m/m0`; initial droplet
  radius comes from the optical/Mie response. The estimated maximum relative error on mass ratio is
  5%. No growth-time dimensions, facet speeds, habit, grain count, or crystallographic orientation
  were directly measured (manuscript pp. 8-10, 23-24).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-08"></a>
- **Sampling:** data were originally recorded at 10-30 Hz. Initial radii were generally below 15
  micrometres, final inferred radii rarely exceeded 60 micrometres, and most traces were shorter
  than 1500 s, with two longer examples (manuscript p. 16, lines 306-311).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-09"></a>
- **Ensemble semantics:** each curve is one selected particle's longitudinal mass history. The set
  is not a habit-frequency survey, and separate curves must not be joined into a synthetic particle
  trajectory (manuscript pp. 16-18; Harrison archive member semantics).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-PROT-10"></a>
- **Morphology limit:** the analysis assumes a small spherical particle for its main transforms and
  explores sensitivity to non-isometric growth in model calculations. Particle shape was not
  observed, so spherical, faceted, single-crystal, and transition labels are analysis hypotheses
  (manuscript pp. 10-14, 23-24).

## 7. Direct observations and derived interpretations

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-OBS-01"></a>
- **Direct:** `m/m0(t)` is the measured quantity and has an estimated maximum relative error of 5%
  (manuscript pp. 8-9, lines 164-169). This error does not provide a distribution, temporal
  covariance, or condition uncertainty.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-OBS-02"></a>
- **Derived target candidate:** normalized/scaled mass growth uses measured mean mass-growth rate
  and direct mass ratio, avoiding direct ambient supersaturation in the transform. It is strictly
  derived under spherical/isometric and constant-condition assumptions and alone does not identify
  diffusion-limited growth (manuscript pp. 11-14).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-OBS-03"></a>
- **Derived target candidate:** the power-law diagnostic is `dm/dt proportional to m^(P/3)`, with
  source reference values `P=1` for diffusion-limited and `P=2` for kinetics-limited growth. It is
  supersaturation-free but requires a registered derivative/smoothing operator (manuscript p. 14,
  lines 272-276).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-OBS-04"></a>
- All seven homogeneous cases assigned a kinetics transition have derived `P<1` throughout growth;
  some other cases begin below one and cross above it. These are derivative-based findings, not raw
  observations, and cannot enter the book until S5 reproduces the operator (manuscript pp. 21-22,
  lines 422-439).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-INT-01"></a>
- **Interpretation/input:** fitting one heterogeneous example gives `alphaHK=0.008 +/- 0.002`,
  compared with the older 2016 fit `0.009 +/- 0.003`. Across heterogeneous cases the revised
  fitted range is 0.008 to 1 (manuscript pp. 17-18). These values are model inversions, not targets.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-INT-02"></a>
- **Interpretation/input:** ten of 17 homogeneous cases are described by constant-mechanism faceted
  fits; seven are not. Faceted-fit `alphaHK` spans 0.004 to 1, while transition fits fall below
  0.002 by the end (manuscript pp. 18-21). This partition depends on DiSKICE and the authors'
  empirical transition model.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-INT-03"></a>
- **Interpretation/input:** the example transition fit changes `alphaHK` from 0.025 to 0.0016.
  Equation 15 is explicitly a qualitative, hypothetical faceting-transition surrogate with
  empirical parameters, so its inferred `s_char` and coefficient history are not observations
  (manuscript p. 20, lines 391-410).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-INT-04"></a>
- **Interpretation/input:** fit-derived `s_char` covers 1.63-34.0% for cases with small fitted
  `alphaHK`. The authors caution that transition-case `s_char` depends on an empirical hypothetical
  model; retain it as mechanism context, not a validation target (manuscript pp. 20, 25).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-CAL-01"></a>
- The prior solution-droplet/2-D chamber calibration gave `sigma_i=28.6 +/- 1.8%`, approximately
  12% below the flat-plate value `32.6 +/- 2%` at the high condition (manuscript p. 28,
  lines 588-590).

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-CAL-02"></a>
- At low plate-temperature difference, the fitted observed minimum was `0.21 +/- 0.01%`; reducing
  modeled uncovered areas by 60% produced a result close to the `0.13%` flat-plate estimate. The
  paper therefore adopts the flat-plate approximation, with the largest revision at low
  `sigma_i` (manuscript pp. 29-30, lines 620-628).

## 9. Limits, conflicts, and exclusions

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-LIM-01"></a>
- Harrison 2016 and Pokrifka 2020 are not independent witnesses. Their difference in low-condition
  `sigma_i` and fitted `alphaHK` is a recalibration/reanalysis of the same raw observations, not a
  cross-laboratory Class-C conflict.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-LIM-02"></a>
- The manuscript's smoothing uses forward/backward low-pass filtering and cubic polynomials; the
  variable window loses data at both ends, and cubic fits are used for the main analysis
  (manuscript pp. 16-17). Phase 8 cannot inherit a derivative or `P` curve without an S5 operator
  and fixtures.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-LIM-03"></a>
- The 5% maximum relative mass-ratio error does not specify correlation across time, a likelihood,
  or uncertainty in derivatives. It must not be repurposed as a confidence interval for `P`,
  `alphaHK`, or `s_char`.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-LIM-04"></a>
- Ambient `sigma_i` uncertainty, missing pressure uncertainty, unreported covariance, and the
  condition-table revision prevent a clean claim that two nominal rows differ only in one input.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-LIM-05"></a>
- The paper's possible single-crystal and faceting-transition narratives are not visually observed.
  Carry the later likely-polycrystalline qualification and leave morphology outside the direct
  target fields.

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-LIM-06"></a>
- Exclude `heticegrowth_625.dat` and do not create a file for the unmatched -31.5 C table row; see
  [`harrison-2016.md`](harrison-2016.md) for the archive-level provenance anomalies.

## 10. Provisional Phase 8 implications - project interpretation

<a id="P8X-POKRIFKA20-P8-01"></a>
- **Witness/robustness:** direct heterogeneous `m/m0(t)` is a provisional Class-B target family from
