## 2. Reading convention and complete protocol tags

`Fact (paraphrase)` is a source-stated fact restated without quotation. `Short quote` is exact
source language and counts toward the 14-word quotation total. `Audit limit` and `provisional
implication` are project judgments, not statements by Bailey and Hallett.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-SEED"></a> **Seed/support - fact (paraphrase):** crystals were
nucleated and grown on clean glass filaments about 50 um in diameter; Figure 3 gives 50-70 um for
the photographed filaments. Kaolinite- and silver-iodide-coated filaments were additionally used
for nucleation-threshold and habit tests (PDF pp. 1, 3-4).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-GEOMETRY"></a> **Transport geometry - fact (paraphrase):**
the horizontal static diffusion chamber used two ice-coated stainless-steel plates separated by a
2.8 cm gap inside an approximately 30 cm diameter acrylic cylinder; width/height exceeded 10:1
(PDF p. 2). The geometry was designed to suppress thermal-edge convection.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-THERMOMETRY"></a> **Temperature field - fact
(paraphrase):** plate temperatures set the chamber profile, and a rotatable/crank thermocouple
measured an approximately linear vertical temperature profile; Figure 1's example uses top -23 C
and bottom -37 C (PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-SIGMA-SEMANTICS"></a> **Supersaturation semantics - fact
(paraphrase):** ice-relative supersaturation was calculated from the two ice-coated plate
temperatures and the assumed/measured chamber profiles; it is a static chamber/environment value,
not a directly measured crystal-surface value (PDF pp. 2-3).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-SIGMA-RANGE"></a> **Supersaturation range - fact
(paraphrase):** the study spans 1% ice supersaturation to values well above water saturation; Figure
3 displays decimal ice supersaturation from 0.01 to 0.60 (PDF pp. 1, 4).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-SIGMA-UNCERTAINTY"></a> **Supersaturation uncertainty -
audit limit:** the precursor prints no numeric temperature, profile-model, or ice-supersaturation
uncertainty and no per-case calibration residual. Preserve uncertainty as `unreported`, not zero.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-PRESSURE"></a> **Pressure - fact (paraphrase):** pressure was
chosen to be commensurate with the standard atmosphere at each stated temperature; Figure 3 spans
approximately 500 to 150 hPa from -20 to -70 C (PDF pp. 1, 3-4). Pressure therefore covaries with
temperature and is not an independently crossed factor in this summary.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-NUCLEATION-PULSE"></a> **Subcritical nucleation history -
fact (paraphrase):** below the glass-filament critical supersaturation (about 25% below -40 C), the
chamber was slowly evacuated just above target pressure and then dropped 5-10 hPa in 1-2 s to
briefly raise supersaturation to nucleate crystals; subsequent growth used the lower preset
supersaturation (PDF p. 3).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-SPONTANEOUS-NUCLEATION"></a> **Above-threshold history -
fact (paraphrase):** at or above the critical supersaturation, the filament was kept near the top-
plate temperature during evacuation and lowered after target pressure was reached, producing
spontaneous nucleation (PDF p. 3).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-COMPETITION"></a> **Competition/selection - fact
(paraphrase):** below water saturation the method produced well-separated crystals; above water
saturation and above -40 C, vapor competition was significant, and only well-separated crystals
were used for growth data (PDF p. 3). This is outcome-based specimen selection, not a full
population census.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-IMAGING"></a> **Imaging - fact (paraphrase):** time-lapse
photography used a video camera and long-working-distance Wild microscope (PDF p. 2). No pixel
scale, cadence, operator rule, or dimensional measurement uncertainty is printed.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-SIZE-WINDOW"></a> **Size/history window - fact
(paraphrase):** photographed crystals ranged from about 70 to 700 um for habit clarity, while
growth rates were calculated from crystals between 100 and 300 um (PDF pp. 3-4).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-ENSEMBLE"></a> **Ensemble semantics - fact (paraphrase):**
crystals were classified by habit; habit-specific rate fits were weighted by habit frequency to
produce averages (PDF pp. 6-8). The precursor supplies neither raw rows nor per-bin sample counts.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PROTOCOL-ERROR-BARS"></a> **Growth-rate uncertainty semantics - short
quote:** error bars "represent the variance in measured growth rates" (PDF p. 8); single-rate
measurement precision is said to be considerably smaller but is not quantified. Variance is not a
standard error or instrument uncertainty.

## 3. Page-cited precursor outcomes and numeric candidates

### 3.1 Habit and nucleation observations

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-HABIT-WARM"></a> **Warm-side summary - fact (paraphrase):** from -20
to -40 C the precursor reports predominantly plate-like, mostly polycrystalline habits (PDF p. 1).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-HABIT-COLD"></a> **Cold-side summary - short quote:** "Columnar growth
occurs predominantly below -40 C" (PDF p. 1); the same passage says sufficiently high ice
supersaturation remains dominated by polycrystalline bullet rosettes.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-NUCLEATION-THRESHOLD"></a> **Nucleation candidate - fact
(paraphrase):** for glass, kaolinite, and silver iodide at reduced pressure below -40 C, about
12%-25% ice supersaturation was reported sufficient for heterogeneous nucleation; glass changes
abruptly near -40 C (PDF p. 3). This is nucleation/input evidence, not a growth-habit target.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-LOW-SIGMA-INVARIANCE"></a> **Low-drive habit candidate - fact
(paraphrase):** Figure 3's 1% and 2% habits are described as representative down to about -60 C
and essentially independent of temperature (PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-HABIT-MINUS20"></a> **At -20 C - fact (paraphrase):** low-to-moderate
drive produced plates and skeletal plates, columns were rare and nearly equiaxed, and increasing
drive produced crossed/parallel-crossed polycrystals; thin sector plates and dorites occurred near
water saturation (PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-HABIT-MINUS30"></a> **At -30 C - fact (paraphrase):** low-drive habit
resembled -20 C with low-frequency short columns of length/width 1.4-2; higher drive produced a
diverse plate/polycrystal family, and the transition toward that complex regime began near -25 C
(PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-HABIT-MINUS40"></a> **Near -40 C - fact (paraphrase):** short columns
had length/width 1.4-2.5; intersecting crossed plates were most frequent between -35 and -40 C;
the precursor places the beginning of plate-to-columnar behavior near -38 C and a fairly sharp
shift just below -40 C, especially near water saturation (PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-HABIT-ROSETTES"></a> **Below -40 C - fact (paraphrase):** bullet
rosettes began near 25% ice supersaturation and increased with drive; columns/bullets typically had
length/width 2-5, and rosettes had 2-6 bullets with 3 most common (PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-HABIT-MINUS50"></a> **At/below -50 C - fact (paraphrase):** long solid
columns occurred at moderate drive, hollow sheaths appeared above 40% ice supersaturation,
needles began near -55 C at moderate drive, and column/needle rosettes began above about 25%
(PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-HABIT-MINUS70"></a> **At -70 C - fact (paraphrase):** needles and needle
rosettes dominated above 25% ice supersaturation; lower drive produced sharply cornered
transitional or sometimes trigonal forms, with occasional solid columns (PDF pp. 5-6).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-POLY-DOMINANCE"></a> **Population summary - fact (paraphrase):**
polycrystals are reported to dominate habit distributions at all presented temperatures and all but
very low ice supersaturation (PDF p. 8). No counts are printed, so the claim cannot be independently
recomputed from the precursor.

### 3.2 Growth-rate, halo, and field-comparison candidates

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-RATE-DEFINITIONS"></a> **Rate observables - fact (paraphrase):** the
precursor reports maximum dimension rate, projected-area rate, and volume rate; maximum
dimension is diameter/width for plate-like habits and length for columns (PDF pp. 1, 6).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-RATE-SPREAD"></a> **Within-condition spread - fact (paraphrase):**
crystals of the same habit at the same ice supersaturation showed a large rate spread associated
with size and aspect-ratio variability (PDF pp. 6-7). Only plotted data and variance bars are given.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-RATE-WEIGHTING"></a> **Average-rate construction - fact
(paraphrase):** the authors fit each habit's rate versus ice supersaturation, weighted those fits by
habit frequency, and interpreted the resulting slope as a growth rate normalized to decimal ice
supersaturation (PDF p. 7).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-WAVE-CLOUD-OBSERVED"></a> **Wave-cloud observation - fact
(paraphrase):** an external DRI Cloudscope case at about -30 C and water saturation observed
50-125 um crystals after about 300 s (PDF p. 8). It is a field comparison, not a second laboratory
witness.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-WAVE-CLOUD-DERIVED"></a> **Wave-cloud calculation - fact
(paraphrase):** the precursor multiplies ice supersaturation 0.33 by normalized rate 0.7 to obtain
0.23 um/s, then predicts about 70 um after 300 s with a 25-120 um spread (PDF p. 8). These values
are derived from a plotted fit and assumptions; they are not independent raw target rows.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PRISTINE-FREQUENCY"></a> **Pristine/halo candidate - fact
(paraphrase):** the highest pristine-crystal frequencies were at no more than about 2% ice
supersaturation (PDF p. 9).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-PRISTINE-TIME"></a> **Pristine size-time candidate - fact
(paraphrase):** reaching 100-200 um at low drive was estimated to require 30-90 min at -30 C and
longer at colder temperatures (PDF p. 9).

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-HALO-RANGE"></a> **Halo-producing range - fact (paraphrase):** the
source suggests pristine populations might persist up to 5% ice supersaturation below -40 C and
possibly to 13% at -60 C (PDF pp. 9-10). The wording is observationally tentative, not a sharp
threshold with uncertainty.

## 5. Conflicts, limits, and provisional Phase 8 implications

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-LIMIT-PRESSURE-CONFOUND"></a> **Pressure confound - audit limit:**
pressure is tied to the standard-atmosphere temperature profile. A temperature trend cannot be
called pressure-independent, and these pages do not provide a same-temperature pressure pair.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-LIMIT-STATIC-VENTILATION"></a> **Geometry transfer limit - fact
(paraphrase):** the paper treats static growth at one supersaturation as approximately equivalent
to ventilated free-fall growth at a lower value, with ventilation effects growing with particle size
(PDF p. 3). That qualitative equivalence is not a measured conversion operator.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-LIMIT-COMPETITION"></a> **Competition/selection limit:** above water
saturation at temperatures warmer than -40 C, only well-separated crystals entered growth data
(PDF p. 3). Habit frequencies and selected growth-rate specimens therefore have different
population semantics unless the later VOR proves otherwise.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-LIMIT-UNCERTAINTY"></a> **Uncertainty limit:** the source supplies no
per-point measurement uncertainty, sample count, fit coefficient table, covariance, or raw data.
Variance bars describe observed spread and cannot be recast as instrument error or confidence
intervals.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-LIMIT-LINEAGE-INDEPENDENCE"></a> **Witness-independence limit:** the
2004, 2009, and 2012 papers are the same Bailey-Hallett laboratory/data lineage, not independent
witnesses merely because they are separate publications. Field comparisons may add a method only
after shared selection and derivation are audited.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-IMPLICATION-COLD-DISAGREEMENT"></a> **Provisional disagreement role:**
the plate/polycrystal regime to near -40 C and cold-side column/rosette shift are candidate Class C
operands against free-fall studies that report a different cold-end habit. The disagreement must
retain pressure, filament, nucleation pulse, static geometry, drive, selection, and polycrystal tags;
it must not be averaged.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-IMPLICATION-TARGETS"></a> **Provisional target role:** observed habit
frequencies, habit-specific rates, and size/time trajectories are target-like outcomes. They remain
unfrozen precursor pointers until current-lineage extraction. Polycrystalline outcomes are also
flagged outside the present single-crystal model class.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-IMPLICATION-INPUTS"></a> **Provisional input role:** nucleation
thresholds and pulse conditions are protocol/input candidates, not validation outcomes for a model
that does not simulate nucleation. Standard-atmosphere pressure is a protocol covariate.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-IMPLICATION-CLASS"></a> **Provisional robustness:** no local 2002
quantity is Class A. At most it is a Class B same-lab precursor or one side of a Class C dispute;
final assignment awaits S3 and the later VOR extraction.

<a id="P8X-BAILEY02-IMPLICATION-SPLIT"></a> **Held-out status:** unassigned in S0. No
Bailey-Hallett entry may be called held out or frozen until Phase 8 S3-S6 completes.
