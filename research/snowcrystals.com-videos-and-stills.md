- **Catalog checked:** 2026-07-14

## What these videos show

These are time-lapse photographs of real ice crystals grown in Libbrecht's laboratory, not
computer simulations. The site says he changes temperature and humidity during growth, so each
final shape reflects its full growth history. The page provides 10 distinct movies and 16 MP4
links when alternate resolutions are counted.

The page gives prose descriptions for only four movie subjects. It does not publish formal
titles for the other six, so those rows are marked **untitled** and retain the site's asset ID.
