## 4. Source-reported protocol tags

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-01"></a>
- **Seed:** ice crystals were generated by adiabatic expansion/cooling of air in a separate small
  chamber and injected with an ice-lined squirt. Seed size, number distribution, and the
  crystallography of the selected seed are not reported (printed p. 844 / PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-02"></a>
- **Population/selection:** a selected crystal was freely suspended for an experiment, but
  polycrystalline products were not excluded; the results report frequent polycrystals below
  approximately −15°C (printed pp. 845–846 / PDF pp. 5–6).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-03"></a>
- **Transport geometry:** a converging vertical cloud-tunnel observation section, 94 cm long,
  17 cm in bottom diameter and 10.2 cm at the top, held the crystal 45 cm above the honeycomb.
  Updraft was adjusted continually to the crystal's terminal fall speed, so the transport state is
  free-fall equivalent with ventilation, not a static chamber (printed p. 843 / PDF p. 3).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-04"></a>
- **Temperature/time:** experiments covered −4 to −20°C at approximately 2°C intervals, with
  growth to 30 min sampled in 5-min increments (printed p. 844 / PDF p. 4; printed p. 854 / PDF
  p. 14).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-05"></a>
- **Temperature history:** over a 30-min run the air temperature rose by 0.4–1.6°C, averaging
  0.8°C. In the high-liquid-water run the rise was 0.9°C by 10 min and 3.7°C by 27.5 min. These
  are measured drifts, not symmetric uncertainty intervals (printed p. 844 / PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-06"></a>
- **Supersaturation semantics:** the environmental condition is nominal saturation relative to
  liquid water. The source gives neither measured ice-relative far-field supersaturation nor
  surface supersaturation, and supplies no humidity uncertainty. It must not be converted here to
  a numeric `sigmaInfinity` value (printed pp. 841, 844 / PDF pp. 1, 4).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-07"></a>
- **Pressure/site:** University of Utah, Salt Lake City, approximately 1300 m above mean sea
  level, at about 860 mb; no pressure uncertainty is stated (printed p. 845 / PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-08"></a>
- **Cloud droplets/liquid water:** ordinary liquid-water content was below 0.5 g m⁻³ over the
  temperature range, with a separate approximately 2 g m⁻³ condition near −10°C. The ordinary
  FSSP distribution peaked near 11 μm, about 5 μm above the impaction-method peak; the high-content
  FSSP distribution peaked near 25 μm. Liquid-water content was inferred from temperature and
  post-evaporation dew point, without a stated uncertainty (printed pp. 844–845 / PDF pp. 4–5).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-09"></a>
- **Substrate interaction:** there was no support substrate during growth. After the requested
  duration, the crystal was collected on a motor-oil-coated cover glass and placed between two
  immiscible oils for microscopy (printed p. 844 / PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-10"></a>
- **Measurement:** top and side photographs were taken except for needles and dendrites; melted-drop
  diameter supplied mass. A hot-wire/Pitot comparison differed by less than 4%, and the stated
  fall-speed error was about 3 cm s⁻¹. No mass or dimension uncertainty and no general specimen
  count are reported (printed p. 844 / PDF p. 4; printed p. 849 / PDF p. 9).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-11"></a>
- **Ensemble semantics:** morphology photographs and the cross-time mass/dimension series use
  different crystals at successive durations. Individual strip-chart fall-speed curves can be
  longitudinal, but the sampled crystals cannot be treated as one growth trajectory (printed
  p. 845 / PDF p. 5; printed pp. 849–850 / PDF pp. 9–10).

<a id="P8X-TF88-PROT-12"></a>
- **Growth-history contamination:** riming commonly began 10–20 min after seeding. At low
  liquid-water content, vapor-dominated growth near −10.5°C persisted to about 10 min; the
  approximately 2 g m⁻³ case entered the reported graupel stage after about 20 min (printed
  pp. 847, 850 / PDF pp. 7, 10).

## 5. Source-reported candidate observations

These lines transcribe numbers or qualitative findings printed in prose. They do not read values
from graph axes or raster table cells.

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-01"></a>
- The study describes maximum sizes on its 30-min scale of about 5 mm diameter for dendrites and
  2 mm length for needles (printed p. 842 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-02"></a>
- The reported broad habit sequence is columns from roughly −4 to −10°C and plates from −10 to
  −20°C; needle enhancement is centered near −5.5°C and dendritic enhancement near −14 to −16°C
  (printed pp. 845–846 / PDF pp. 5–6).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-03"></a>
- Isometric products occurred near −4°C and from −8 to −11.5°C (printed p. 847 / PDF p. 7).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-04"></a>
- c-axis growth was strongest near −5.5°C and a-axis growth near −15°C. Mass had a maximum near
  −15°C and, after 10 min, another maximum near −10°C attributed by the authors to riming
  (printed pp. 847–848 / PDF pp. 7–8).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-05"></a>
- Fall speed had minima near −5.5 and −16°C and a maximum near −10°C (printed p. 849 / PDF p. 9).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-06"></a>
- At −10.5°C the early fall-speed acceleration was `0.056 ± 0.003 cm s⁻²`; a bend occurred at
  8–9 min. The separate measurement error was about 3 cm s⁻¹ (printed p. 849 / PDF p. 9).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-07"></a>
- At −10.5°C the printed log-mass/log-time slopes were 1.6 from 0–6 min, 2.3 from 6–20 min, and
  6.2 after 20 min. The paper identifies these intervals respectively as vapor-diffusional,
  transitional/riming, and high-liquid-water graupel growth (printed p. 850 / PDF p. 10).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-08"></a>
- The conclusion summarizes the same three mass regimes approximately as proportional to
  `t^(3/2)`, `t^2`, and `t^6`. Only `t^(3/2)` belongs to the vapor-diffusional regime; the latter
  two must not become bare-vapor solver targets (printed p. 854 / PDF p. 14).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-09"></a>
- Apparent density around −14°C decreased until about 10 min and then remained approximately
  constant. The source defines this as mass divided by a circumscribing hexagonal-cylinder volume,
  not material ice density (printed pp. 848, 851 / PDF pp. 8, 11).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-10"></a>
- The still-air fall-distance estimates are 200–250 m at 15 min and 700–800 m at 30 min for the
  isometric/near-spherical group, versus 125–150 m and 300–400 m for the nonspherical group
  (printed p. 852 / PDF p. 12).

<a id="P8X-TF88-OBS-11"></a>
- The authors associate riming onset with Reynolds number above about 5; their conclusion places
  the graupel state near Reynolds number 100 (printed pp. 853–854 / PDF pp. 13–14).

## 7. Source limits and conflicts

<a id="P8X-TF88-LIM-01"></a>
- The source supplies no general sample counts, mass/dimension error model, or confidence
  intervals. Reported temperature drift, fall-speed error, and hot-wire/Pitot disagreement must
  not be generalized into uncertainty for other observables.

<a id="P8X-TF88-LIM-02"></a>
- Nominal water saturation, inferred liquid-water content, and a changing temperature do not define
  a constant numeric ice supersaturation history.

<a id="P8X-TF88-LIM-03"></a>
- Riming, graupel, ventilation, and polycrystallinity are load-bearing for substantial parts of the
  run. A vapor-only single-prism simulation cannot silently absorb them into a larger tolerance.

<a id="P8X-TF88-LIM-04"></a>
- The 1991 pressure comparison reuses these 860-mb measurements but changes site, tunnel, fog
  production, liquid-water content, thermal stability, selection policy, and crystallinity scope;
  pressure is not the only changed variable.

## 8. Provisional Phase 8 implications — project interpretation, not source claims

<a id="P8X-TF88-INT-01"></a>
- **Pressure:** classify the 860-versus-1010-mb comparison as Class-B/context and confounded, not a
  clean quantitative diffusivity target.

<a id="P8X-TF88-INT-02"></a>
- **Potential targets:** early vapor-regime mass exponent, habit band, dimensions, apparent
  density, and fall-speed observables are candidates only after matching seed, pressure,
  free-fall/ventilation, temperature history, and ensemble semantics.

<a id="P8X-TF88-INT-03"></a>
- **Out-of-model tags:** rimed, graupel, and polycrystalline observations should be retained in the
  book as real phenomena but marked outside the current bare vapor-growth model class.

<a id="P8X-TF88-INT-04"></a>
- **No synthetic trajectory:** cross-time morphology, mass, and dimensions are ensemble snapshots;
  do not join them into one crystal history or pair independently sampled dimensions across times.

<a id="P8X-TF88-INT-05"></a>
- These interpretations remain provisional until Phase 8 S1–S4 normalize supersaturation,
  protocol tags, witness classes, and the input/target partition. This extraction alone grants no
  validation claim.
