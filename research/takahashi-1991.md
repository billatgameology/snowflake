## 2. Currency, lineage, and rights

### Source facts

<a id="P8X-T91-CUR-01"></a>
- The article states that it continues and extends Takahashi and Fukuta 1988, with more detailed
  measurements, a broader −3 to −23°C range, modified tunnel hardware, and an isolated
  single-crystal population excluding polycrystalline growth (printed p. 15 / PDF p. 1).

<a id="P8X-T91-CUR-02"></a>
- Fukuta and Takahashi 1999, *The Growth of Atmospheric Ice Crystals: A Summary of Findings in
  Vertical Supercooled Cloud Tunnel Studies*, DOI
  [`10.1175/1520-0469(1999)056<1963:TGOAIC>2.0.CO;2`](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0469%281999%29056%3C1963%3ATGOAIC%3E2.0.CO%3B2),
  is a later authorial synthesis of the same tunnel program, not an independent repetition.

<a id="P8X-T91-CUR-03"></a>
- Westbrook and Heymsfield 2011, *Ice Crystals Growing from Vapor in Supercooled Clouds between
  −2.5° and −22°C*, DOI [`10.1175/JAS-D-11-017.1`](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-11-017.1),
  is a later independent reanalysis that includes the 1991 data; it is interpretation, not a
  replacement version of the experiment.

<a id="P8X-T91-CUR-04"></a>
- Takahashi 2014, *Influence of Liquid Water Content and Temperature on the Form and Growth of
  Branched Planar Snow Crystals in a Cloud*, DOI
  [`10.1175/JAS-D-14-0043.1`](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-14-0043.1), reports a new,
  narrower branched-planar experiment and does not supersede the full 1991 range.

<a id="P8X-T91-CUR-05"></a>
- The publisher/author-lineage search through 2026-08-10 found the integrated 1991 corrigendum but
  no later official correction or replacement. This records the bounded search outcome rather
  than asserting an exhaustive survey of world literature.

<a id="P8X-T91-RIGHTS-01"></a>
- Both J-STAGE records are free access, but display no item-specific Creative Commons license. The
  article bears Meteorological Society of Japan copyright. The
  [J-STAGE terms for individuals](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/static/pages/TermsAndPolicies/ForIndividuals/-char/en)
  do not turn free reading into permission to republish page scans, figures, raster tables, or a
  derivative database; retain only brief cited extracts unless permission or a legal exception
  applies.

## 5. Source-reported protocol tags

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-01"></a>
- **Apparatus/transport:** a modified vertical supercooled cloud tunnel freely suspended a crystal
  in an updraft equivalent to free fall. Relative to 1988, an ultrasonic atomizer replaced steam
  fog generation and commercial air filters formed the suction chambers. The transport includes
  ventilation and is not a static chamber (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-02"></a>
- **Population/selection:** isolated single crystals were grown individually; polycrystalline
  growth was explicitly excluded (printed p. 15 / PDF p. 1).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-03"></a>
- **Seed:** ice crystals were generated by adiabatic cooling of air, as in the 1988 experiment.
  Individual seed size and crystallography are not reported. The authors note that this method was
  known to yield predominantly scalene-hexagonal and trigonal crystals above −9°C (printed p. 16 /
  PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-04"></a>
- **Temperature/time:** the stated range is −3 to −23°C and 3–30 min, with detailed measurements
  at ten temperatures spanning −3.7 to −22.0°C (printed pp. 15–16 / PDF pp. 1–2).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-05"></a>
- **Temperature uncertainty/history:** average fluctuation was ±0.2°C, with stated standard
  deviation 0.1°C, through 10 min and ±0.4°C over 20–30 min. The average run-to-run temperature
  difference standard deviation was 0.2°C (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-06"></a>
- **Supersaturation semantics:** the source condition is nominal saturation relative to liquid
  water. It is not a measured ice-relative far-field or surface supersaturation, and no humidity
  uncertainty is reported. It must not be converted here to a numeric `sigmaInfinity` value
  (printed pp. 15–16 / PDF pp. 1–2).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-07"></a>
- **Pressure/site:** Hokkaido University, Sapporo, average atmospheric pressure about 1010 mb;
  no pressure uncertainty is stated (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-08"></a>
- **Cloud droplets/liquid water:** impaction measurements gave an average droplet diameter of about
  8 μm, with most droplets between 5 and 15 μm; liquid-water content was about 0.1 g m⁻³ and has no
  stated uncertainty (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-09"></a>
- **Substrate interaction:** there was no support substrate during growth. After collection, a
  crystal was held between heavy oils for microscopic photography (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-10"></a>
- **Measurement:** top and side photographs were both taken only for isometric crystals. For a
  planar crystal other than a thick plate, c-axis length was inferred from mass divided by basal
  area under an assumed bulk density. Melted-drop diameter supplied mass; a digitizer supplied
  basal area and perimeter (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-11"></a>
- **Fall-speed measurement:** fog temperature and updraft were monitored with a thermistor and
  hot-wire anemometer/strip chart. Estimated fall-speed accuracy was ±3 cm s⁻¹ (printed pp. 16, 21 /
  PDF pp. 2, 7).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-12"></a>
- **Ensemble semantics:** successive photographic durations are different specimens, not repeated
  observations of one crystal. Figure 11 reports averages of four measurement sets, and Table 4
  says each relationship averages four measurements (printed pp. 16, 22, 25 / PDF pp. 2, 8, 11).

<a id="P8X-T91-PROT-13"></a>
- **Growth-history restriction:** except at −5.3 and −14.4°C, runs were generally restricted to
  12–20 min because riming commenced afterward (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 2).

## 6. Source-reported candidate observations

These are prose/caption facts, not values read from plotted curves or raster table cells.

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-01"></a>
- The broad habit sequence is plates above −4.0°C, columns from −4.0 to −8.1°C, plates from −8.1
  to −22.4°C, and columns below −22.4°C (printed pp. 15, 29 / PDF pp. 1, 15).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-02"></a>
- Time-dependent shape enhancement is reported near −5.5, −12, −14.5, −16.5, and −18°C
  (printed pp. 15, 29 / PDF pp. 1, 15).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-03"></a>
- Under nominal water saturation, dendrites occurred from −14.1 to −15.8°C. The authors contrast
  this with cited static-chamber experiments requiring several percent supersaturation relative to
  liquid water (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-04"></a>
- At 10 min the axial ratio reached a maximum near −14.8°C and a minimum near −5.8°C, spanning a
  factor of about 1000. Reported isometric ranges were above −4.4°C, −6.4 to −11.3°C, and below
  −18.4°C (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 2).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-05"></a>
- Time markers in the morphology account include hollow-column edge growth by 3 min at −5.3°C,
  plate-to-sector transition after 10 min at −12.2°C, broad-branch-to-dendrite transition after
  5 min at −14.4°C, and corner extensions after 7 min at −18.2°C (printed pp. 16–18 / PDF
  pp. 2–4).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-06"></a>
- All reported planar specimens were double plates. Six branches on the larger side occurred with
  reported probabilities of 43% at −12.2°C, 76% at −14.4°C, and 63% at −16.5°C; denominators and
  confidence intervals are absent (printed p. 18 / PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-07"></a>
- Mass maxima occurred near −5.8 and −14.8°C; the −5.8°C maximum was obscure before 5 min
  (printed p. 18 / PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-08"></a>
- The mass-time exponent was approximately 1.5 for isometric crystals at −3.7, −8.6, −10.6,
  −20.1, and −22.0°C. Shape-enhanced slopes exceeded 1.9 at −5.3°C and, after the reported onset,
  at −12.2, −14.4, and −16.5°C (printed pp. 18, 29 / PDF pp. 4, 15).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-09"></a>
- At 30 min the reported maximum dendrite diameter was about 4 mm and needle length about 2 mm
  (printed p. 19 / PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-10"></a>
- c-axis growth was strongest near −5.8°C and a-axis growth near −14.8°C; the respective opposite
  minima were c-axis near −14.8°C and a-axis near −4.0°C. Isometric a- and c-axis lengths each
  scaled approximately as `t^0.5` (printed p. 19 / PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-11"></a>
- The authors attribute inflections near −11 and −19°C in c-axis results to a change in measurement
  method at thick-plate/plate boundaries; they are not clean habit-transition observations
  (printed p. 19 / PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-12"></a>
- Apparent density at −14.4°C became approximately constant after maximum dimension exceeded
  0.9 mm. It continued decreasing beyond 0.3 mm at −12.2, −16.5, and −18.2°C; at −5.3°C it was
  already approximately constant after early hollowing (printed p. 20 / PDF p. 6).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-13"></a>
- Fall speed was greatest near −9.2°C, had a second peak near −19°C, and had minima near −4.5 and
  −14.5°C. For isometric crystals it was roughly proportional to maximum dimension to the 1.5
  power (printed pp. 21–23 / PDF pp. 7–9).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-14"></a>
- Ventilation became noticeable after 7 min at −12.2°C, 4 min at −14.4°C, and 5 min at −16.5°C.
  Reported Reynolds-number thresholds were about 2 for the −12.2/−16.5°C sectors and about 5 for
  the −14.4°C dendrite (printed pp. 24–26 / PDF pp. 10–12).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-15"></a>
- Reynolds number stayed below 4.5 for the isometric cases and reached 7.5 at −18.2°C without a
  reported ventilation effect (printed p. 26 / PDF p. 12).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-16"></a>
- Thick plates and plates behaved approximately as Stokes particles below Reynolds number 5; the
  drag-law slope became gentler above 5, where shape enhancement occurred (printed pp. 28–29 /
  PDF pp. 14–15).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-17"></a>
- The −5.3°C needle axial ratio changed from about 0.7 at 3 min to 0.07 at 30 min (printed p. 28 /
  PDF p. 14).

<a id="P8X-T91-OBS-18"></a>
- The cross-paper comparison reports crystals at 860 mb approximately 30% heavier after 10 min
  than crystals at 1010 mb; the accompanying spherical calculation predicts a 20–30% difference
  (printed pp. 28–29 / PDF pp. 14–15).

## 8. Source limits and conflicts

<a id="P8X-T91-LIM-01"></a>
- Apart from temperature stability and ±3 cm s⁻¹ fall-speed accuracy, the article provides few
  quantitative uncertainty bounds. Most specimen counts, probability denominators, mass error,
  dimension error, and fit-coefficient uncertainty are absent.

<a id="P8X-T91-LIM-02"></a>
- Nominal water saturation plus a droplet population does not specify a constant numeric
  ice-relative supersaturation, still less a surface supersaturation.

<a id="P8X-T91-LIM-03"></a>
- The a- and c-axis series are ensemble summaries, and planar c-axis values are partly inferred by
  a different method. The source does not establish row-wise pairing suitable for constructing a
  synthetic per-crystal volume or aspect-ratio trajectory; Phase 8 must treat those dimensions as
  unpaired unless a separately registered extraction proves otherwise.

<a id="P8X-T91-LIM-04"></a>
- Apparent density is mass divided by a circumscribing hexagonal-cylinder volume. It is a derived
  morphology observable, not material density.

<a id="P8X-T91-LIM-05"></a>
- Riming truncates most longer runs, while free-fall ventilation affects several planar cases.
  Neither effect can be hidden in an undifferentiated vapor-growth target.

<a id="P8X-T91-LIM-06"></a>
- The pressure comparison changes more than pressure: site, tunnel implementation, fog generation,
  liquid-water content, temperature drift, seed/population policy, polycrystal exclusion, riming,
  and ventilation context differ between the two source experiments.

<a id="P8X-T91-LIM-07"></a>
- `research/phase6-source-currency.md` preserves an earlier Table 2 transcription for a diagnostic.
  This S0 index does not independently adopt or validate those raster-cell values. Any Phase 8 book
  use of a table coefficient remains a separate registered digitization with read uncertainty.

## 9. Provisional Phase 8 implications — project interpretation, not source claims

<a id="P8X-T91-INT-01"></a>
- **Witness class:** this paper supplies one experimental-lineage witness. Its warm habit boundaries
  can contribute to a Class-A claim only alongside a different laboratory and method with matching
  protocol tags.

<a id="P8X-T91-INT-02"></a>
- **Cold conflict:** the reported columns below −22.4°C are a provisional Class-C position against
  cold plate/polycrystal reports from other protocols. The rapid-cooling seed and deliberate
  exclusion of polycrystals are candidate reconciliation variables, not a settled explanation.

<a id="P8X-T91-INT-03"></a>
- **Pressure:** the 860-versus-1010-mb result is Class-B/context and confounded, not a clean
  quantitative pressure/diffusivity target.

<a id="P8X-T91-INT-04"></a>
- **Potential external targets:** mass exponent, separately reported dimensions, size-dependent
  apparent density, fall speed, and time-marked morphology may be candidates after protocol and
  held-out audits. Habit category is partly in-sample for a Nakaya-tuned model and needs an
  explicit input-versus-target label.

<a id="P8X-T91-INT-05"></a>
- **Current-model mismatch:** ventilation/drag, riming, double-plate asymmetry, hollowing, and the
  source's seed morphology require either explicit operators/modules or out-of-model flags.

<a id="P8X-T91-INT-06"></a>
- **Ensemble rule:** do not turn time-separated photographs or unpaired a/c dimensions into one
  continuous crystal history. A target operator must score the reported ensemble semantics or use
  only genuinely longitudinal records.

<a id="P8X-T91-INT-07"></a>
- These implications remain provisional until Phase 8 S1–S4 normalize supersaturation, protocol
  comparability, witness classes, and the input/target partition. This extraction and its
  corrigendum integration do not grant a validation label.
