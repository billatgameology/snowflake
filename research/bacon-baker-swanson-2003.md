## 2. Reading convention and complete protocol tags

`Fact (paraphrase)` is a source-stated fact restated without quotation. `Short quote` is exact
source language and counts toward the 19-word quotation total. `Audit limit` and `provisional
implication` are project judgments, not source statements.

### 2.1 Geometry, measurements, and environment

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-GEOMETRY"></a> **Transport geometry - fact (paraphrase):** an
electrodynamic balance held and followed one charged particle at a time near the center of a
temperature-controlled, sealed thermal diffusion chamber, without a physical substrate (printed
pp. 1905-1906 / PDF pp. 3-4).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-INTERACTIONS"></a> **Interaction/ventilation semantics - fact
(paraphrase):** the source says electrodynamic trapping cannot capture particle-particle
interactions and provides no ventilation-effect information (printed p. 1905 / PDF p. 3). Tag this as
levitated with no imposed free-fall ventilation, not as proven zero relative airflow.

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-PRESSURE"></a> **Pressure/background gas - fact (paraphrase):**
all reported observations were at 1 atmosphere pressure in air (printed pp. 1904 and 1908 / PDF
pp. 2 and 6).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-CAMERAS"></a> **Optical geometry - fact (paraphrase):** two
telemicroscope video cameras viewed each particle 90 degrees apart with pulsed backlighting
(printed p. 1906 / PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-SIZE-UNCERTAINTY"></a> **Size uncertainty - fact
(paraphrase):** water-droplet calibration gave about +/-5% overall effective-size accuracy and an
optical resolution limit of about +/-2 um (printed p. 1906 / PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-ASPECT-UNCERTAINTY"></a> **Aspect-ratio uncertainty - fact
(paraphrase):** aspect ratio was operationally longest c-direction dimension divided by longest
a-direction dimension, with estimated error +/-0.2 due to uncertain camera alignment (printed
p. 1911 / PDF p. 9).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-TEMPERATURE-GRADIENT"></a> **Humidity-control gradient - fact
(paraphrase):** the upper/lower ice-surface temperature difference controlled saturation; at least
15 C difference was needed for ice saturation and most growth runs used more than 20 C (printed
p. 1908 / PDF p. 6).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-SIGMA-SEMANTICS"></a> **Supersaturation semantics - fact
(paraphrase):** ambient ice-relative supersaturation at chamber center was not determined from
plate temperatures because frost on ring electrodes acted as a vapor sink. It was inferred from the
same particle's measured mass-growth or sublimation curve by fitting a spherical heat/vapor-
diffusion model (printed p. 1908 / PDF p. 6).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-SIGMA-VARIATION"></a> **Runwise supersaturation variation -
fact (paraphrase):** one best-fit ambient supersaturation described each curve; its variation over a
growth curve was typically 0.5%-2%, depending on conditions (printed p. 1908 / PDF p. 6). The
source does not define this as a confidence interval.

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-SIGMA-CROSSCHECK"></a> **Humidity cross-check - fact
(paraphrase):** when pure-water drops did not freeze, their evaporation implied humidity agreeing
with ice-particle growth under similar conditions within +/-2% in saturation, explicitly 0.02 in
saturation ratio (printed p. 1908 / PDF p. 6).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-ELECTRIC-FIELD"></a> **Electric-field/charge control - fact
(paraphrase):** the local DC field was typically below 10,000 V/m; no noticeable rate or morphology
difference was attributed to charge from 0.08 to 0.8 pC, but electric-field effects could not be
excluded (printed p. 1909 / PDF p. 7).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-CONTAMINATION"></a> **Contamination control - fact
(paraphrase):** changing cryogenic-fluid types produced no detected systematic morphology
difference (printed p. 1909 / PDF p. 7). This is a bounded comparison, not proof of contaminant-
free conditions.

### 2.2 Initiation and growth histories

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-DROPLET-HV"></a> **Droplet method 1 - fact (paraphrase):** a
5 kV pulse at a syringe needle generated 30-60 um droplets carrying about 0.5 pC (printed p. 1906 /
PDF p. 4).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-DROPLET-INKJET"></a> **Droplet method 2 - fact (paraphrase):**
an inkjet source generated droplets of about 20 um radius; a -500 V grid about 1 mm from the
nozzle induced charge of order 0.1 pC (printed pp. 1906-1907 / PDF pp. 4-5).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-HOMOGENEOUS"></a> **Low-temperature droplet initiation - fact
(paraphrase):** HPLC-grade water drops were injected below -35 C, then the upper plate was heated
and growth temperature stabilized after a few minutes; these particles were grown mostly below
-30 C and were called homogeneous initiation (printed p. 1907 / PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-HETEROGENEOUS"></a> **Higher-temperature droplet initiation -
fact (paraphrase):** SNOMAX-containing water was used for heterogeneous freezing; preparation was
about 0.01 g SNOMAX per liter, estimated at about 1e-13 g and several bacterial cells per 20 um-
radius droplet (printed p. 1907 / PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-FREEZING-OBSERVATION"></a> **Freezing observation limit - fact
(paraphrase):** freezing occurred before focus and was not witnessed in detail; particles were
classified frozen by shape and subsequent growth under ice-supersaturated/water-undersaturated
conditions (printed p. 1907 / PDF p. 5).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-FROST-PARTICLE"></a> **Frost-particle history - fact
(paraphrase):** chamber-wall frost fragments were mechanically flicked into the balance; charges
were 0.1-0.5 pC and growth temperatures were preset (printed p. 1907 / PDF p. 5). They were
generally irregular/polycrystalline rather than a single-crystal seed class.

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-FROST-SEED"></a> **Frost-seed history - fact (paraphrase):** a
trapped frost fragment was infrared-heated and sublimated for about 20 min to below 10 um, then
usually held subsaturated at least 5 more minutes before growth began (printed pp. 1907-1908 / PDF
pp. 5-6).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-SEED-RESIDUAL"></a> **Seed-state uncertainty - fact
(paraphrase):** mass stopped changing during the extra subsaturated hold, but the authors did not
know whether ice remained in a crack or on the seed surface (printed p. 1908 / PDF p. 6).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-ENSEMBLE"></a> **Ensemble semantics - short quote:** "We
followed the evolution of over 100 ice particles" (printed p. 1909 / PDF p. 7). These are
longitudinal single-particle histories across multiple initiation classes, not an equal-count grid or
published population-frequency table.

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-DOMAIN-STATED"></a> **Stated experiment domain - fact
(paraphrase):** the Results section says more than 100 particles were followed from -4 to -38 C,
from 0% to 10% ice supersaturation, always below water saturation, to about 100-200 um final size
(printed p. 1909 / PDF p. 7). Section 5 records why this range cannot be accepted without conflict.

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-CLASSIFICATION"></a> **Morphology classification - fact
(paraphrase):** final particles were grouped as compact hexagonal, florid with thin plate-like side
planes, or polycrystalline; later transition analysis used a rough visual compact/florid criterion
(printed pp. 1910 and 1918 / PDF pp. 8 and 16).

<a id="P8X-BACON-PROTOCOL-EXCLUSIONS"></a> **Aspect-ratio selection - fact (paraphrase):**
florid crystals, polycrystals, and frost particles were excluded from the aspect-ratio analysis;
this removed most particles below about -22 C (printed pp. 1911-1912 / PDF pp. 9-10).

## 3. Page-cited outcomes and numeric candidates

### 3.1 Supersaturation-free and morphology observations

<a id="P8X-BACON-NONUNIQUENESS"></a> **Central morphology result - short quote:**
"morphology is not uniquely determined by environmental temperature and humidity" (printed
p. 1903 / PDF p. 1). The source attributes additional dependence to initiation/history; the quote
does not mean temperature and humidity have no influence.

<a id="P8X-BACON-RADIUS-SQRT-TIME"></a> **Supersaturation-free scaling candidate - fact
(paraphrase):** for particles grown from frost, frost seeds, and droplets, spherical-equivalent
radius varied as time to the one-half power within the experimental errors, for growth and
sublimation and independent of final compact/column/plate/florid/polycrystal morphology (printed
pp. 1910-1911 / PDF pp. 8-9). The source provides no fitted exponent uncertainty in this paper.

<a id="P8X-BACON-DROPLET-ASPECT"></a> **Frozen-droplet compact aspect ratios - fact
(paraphrase):** Table 1 summarizes aspect ratios from 0.6 to 1.5, with no significant temperature
dependence above -22 C; plates dominate below -22 C, more florid forms occur at higher ambient
supersaturation, and faces are rough (printed p. 1912 / PDF p. 10).

<a id="P8X-BACON-SEED-ASPECT"></a> **Frost-seed compact aspect ratios - fact
(paraphrase):** Table 1 summarizes a 0.4-8 range with few values between 0.8 and 1.2, no detected
temperature or ambient-supersaturation dependence, very flat faces, and no florid forms (printed
p. 1912 / PDF p. 10).

<a id="P8X-BACON-DROPLET-BISTABILITY"></a> **Same-condition variability candidate - fact
(paraphrase):** compact droplet-grown particles showed no significant primary-habit dependence on
temperature, vapor excess, or supersaturation over -4 to -38 C; thick plates and short columns
sometimes occurred at the same temperature and humidity (printed p. 1912 / PDF p. 10).

<a id="P8X-BACON-SEED-MORPHOLOGY"></a> **Seed-history candidate - fact (paraphrase):**
below-10 um seed particles tended to grow into prism-like single crystals at all temperatures, with
sharper/flatter faces and a wide variety of aspect ratios under similar temperature and
supersaturation; polycrystalline and florid forms were seldom observed (printed p. 1913 / PDF
p. 11). The single-crystal label is morphological inference, not diffraction.

<a id="P8X-BACON-POLY-TEMPERATURE"></a> **Polycrystal candidate - fact (paraphrase):**
droplets below about -22 C froze mainly as polycrystals; all particles grown directly from frost
were polycrystalline (printed pp. 1911, 1921 / PDF pp. 9, 19).

<a id="P8X-BACON-SIDEPLANE-DOMAIN"></a> **Side-plane domain - fact (paraphrase):** side
planes were observed from -38 to -13 C; a capped-plate morphology was common near -14 C even at
moderate supersaturation (printed p. 1914 / PDF p. 12).

<a id="P8X-BACON-FLORID-EXCESS"></a> **Florid-transition candidate - fact (paraphrase):**
florid crystals appeared preferentially above vapor-density excess about 0.05 g/m3; compact and
florid particles overlapped and the transition was gradual rather than sharp (printed p. 1915 /
PDF p. 13). Vapor-density excess is not interchangeable with a fixed dimensionless percentage
across temperatures.

<a id="P8X-BACON-FLORID-UPPER-TRANSITIONS"></a> **Observed upper transitions - fact
(paraphrase):** Table 2 gives ambient ice supersaturation upper transitions of 11% at -15 C, 20%
at -20 C, and 14% at -25 C (printed p. 1921 / PDF p. 19). They delimit a visually classified,
mixed transition and carry no printed statistical uncertainty.

<a id="P8X-BACON-SIZE-TREND"></a> **Size trend - fact (paraphrase):** Figures 10-11 show a
weak tendency for larger particles to become florid at lower supersaturation, but the source says
scatter is large and size alone does not explain the mixture (printed p. 1921 / PDF p. 19).

<a id="P8X-BACON-FIG4-CONDITIONS"></a> **Figure 4 image set - fact (paraphrase):** compact

<a id="P8X-BACON-FIG5-CONDITIONS"></a> **Figure 5 image set - fact (paraphrase):** florid

<a id="P8X-BACON-FIG8-HISTORY"></a> **Figure 8 history - fact (paraphrase):** a rosette-like

<a id="P8X-BACON-MASS-FACTOR-DROPLETS"></a> **Droplet mass-history candidate - fact
(paraphrase):** Figure 6 particles gained mass by 11.2 +/-4.5 on average, minimum 5.1; the authors
argue those gains were sufficient for facet-rate anisotropy to have produced stronger aspect-ratio

<a id="P8X-BACON-MASS-FACTOR-SEEDS"></a> **Seed mass history - fact (paraphrase):** seed-

### 3.3 Source-model inferences - not direct targets

<a id="P8X-BACON-CRITICAL-SIGMA-INFERRED"></a> **Inferred critical surface values - fact
(paraphrase):** Table 2 converts the ambient upper transitions through a Wood et al. model into
lower limits for critical prism-facet layer-nucleation supersaturation: at least 2.4% at -15 C,
4.4% at -20 C, and 3.1% at -25 C (printed pp. 1919-1921 / PDF pp. 17-19). These are model-
dependent input/interpretation candidates, not observed ambient targets.

<a id="P8X-BACON-DISLOCATION-INFERENCE"></a> **Defect-class inference - fact
(paraphrase):** the paper infers droplet-grown crystals were probably defect-rich on both facet
families and frost-seed crystals likely had defects on one facet family, based on morphology and
growth behavior (printed pp. 1915-1916 / PDF pp. 13-14). Defects and dislocations were not
measured directly.

<a id="P8X-BACON-MODEL-RATIO"></a> **Transition conversion operand - fact (paraphrase):**
the conversion assumes a model ratio of ambient to critical surface supersaturation about 4.2 for
100 um-scale crystals and little size dependence (printed p. 1919 / PDF p. 17). A model adopting
that operand cannot validate itself against the derived critical values.

## 5. Conflicts, limits, and provisional Phase 8 implications

<a id="P8X-BACON-CONFLICT-SIGMA-RANGE"></a> **Unresolved source-internal range conflict -
hard preservation rule:** the Results prose states 0%-10% ice supersaturation (printed p. 1909 /
PDF p. 7), but Figure 5's caption states 4%-18% (printed p. 1912 / PDF p. 10) and Figure 9 visibly
contains points above 20% (printed p. 1915 / PDF p. 13). Phase 8 must preserve `stated prose range
= 0%-10%; figure evidence extends beyond it` and must not normalize the study to one invented
range.

<a id="P8X-BACON-LIMIT-SIGMA-CIRCULARITY"></a> **Supersaturation circularity - audit limit:**
ambient supersaturation is inferred from the same particle mass curve used to discuss growth.
Absolute rate-amplitude comparisons therefore share an inversion; the time exponent and
morphology conditional on reported supersaturation are less circular but retain calibration
uncertainty.

<a id="P8X-BACON-LIMIT-SINGLE-CRYSTAL"></a> **Crystal-state limit:** single-crystal and
defect-class labels are based on optical appearance/model reasoning, not crystallography. The 2024
reanalysis calls its selected subset presumably single-crystal and warns about undetected hollowing
and polycrystal/model limitations.

<a id="P8X-BACON-LIMIT-VENTILATION"></a> **Levitation-motion limit:** no free-fall
ventilation was imposed, but the source does not measure ventilation effects and the 2019 apparatus
paper notes rapid levitated-particle motion. Do not tag the transport as demonstrated stagnant air.

<a id="P8X-BACON-LIMIT-VISUAL-CLASSIFIER"></a> **Classifier/selection limit:** compact versus
florid is explicitly a rough visual criterion; Figure 9 omits transitional and rosette forms, and the
aspect-ratio study omits florid/polycrystalline/frost particles. These subsets cannot be treated as
unselected population frequencies.

<a id="P8X-BACON-LIMIT-HISTORY"></a> **History limit:** homogeneous droplets underwent a
temperature change after freezing, seeds underwent about 20 min sublimation plus a hold, and
droplet/seed mass-growth factors differ by roughly an order of magnitude. Temperature/humidity
alone are not matched protocol tags.
