# Plan — Pre-adoption Phase 9 knowledge baseline

- **Phase:** Research preparation for proposed Phase 9 — no implementation or experiment authority
- **Status:** in progress
- **Started:** 2026-08-12
- **Last touched:** 2026-08-12 by OpenAI Codex

## Goal

Build the smallest defensible knowledge base needed to turn the completed Phase 8 measurements into
Phase 9 calculations and falsifiable experiments. The deliverable is not another measurement hunt:
it is a source-grounded map from competing crystal-growth mechanisms to equations, assumptions,
calculable predictions, decisive Phase 8 records, and outcomes that would reject each hypothesis.

This work is research-only preparation. It does not adopt Phase 9, change either permanent solver,
run a model comparison, or grant any validation claim.

## Done when

The preparation is complete when:

1. the OpenAlex lead *The quantitative growth law of ice crystals and its new model* is identified,
   acquired if legitimately available, and adjudicated for current scientific value;
2. every proposed arm in
   [phase-9-modular-physics-arms.md](phase-9-modular-physics-arms.md) has a compact theory capsule
   naming its governing relation, variables, assumptions, expected observable effect, strongest
   counter-hypothesis, applicable Phase 8 record IDs, and a result that would count against it;
3. every load-bearing equation and numeric constant has a primary-source locator and status
   (`printed`, `derived`, `fit`, `digitized`, or `project hypothesis`), with copyrighted source bytes
   and rendered equation pages retained on the NAS rather than committed;
4. each calculation used to rank an experiment has a replayable script or explicit hand derivation,
   named inputs, units, output, and a check against a printed limiting case or example where one is
   available;
5. a readable guide and a structured hypothesis matrix distinguish established physics, competing
   interpretations, project calculations, unknowns, and predictions that Phase 8 cannot identify;
6. source currency is checked through 2026-08-12 for every promoted mechanism lineage, an
   adversarial interpretation audit has no unresolved blocker, and tracked verification is green.

Completeness means coverage of the proposed decision space, not a claim that the world literature
has been exhausted.

## Approach

Treat a paper as useful only when it changes an equation, assumption, prediction, discriminator, or
uncertainty. Begin with sources already archived and indexed, especially the Libbrecht lineage,
Pokrifka–Harrington lineage, Nelson shape/resistance work, and the 2026 step-source paper. Then do a
small source-currency pass around the exact OpenAlex model lead and named gaps in the module shelf.

The search has three bounded query families: surface/shape-growth laws; coupled vapour, heat,
pressure, and ventilation transport; and initiation/history mechanisms. Use captured OpenAlex data
before a live request, inspect only the first small result page, follow exact author/citation
lineages deliberately, and issue sequential requests without automatic retries. Do not reopen the
Phase 8 residual backlog. Save any acquired PDF and derived page images under
`/Volumes/snowcrystal/research-cache/content/`; if the NAS disconnects, stage new bytes on local Mac
storage and move plus hash-verify them after reconnection.

The extraction unit is a **theory capsule**, not a prose paper summary:

- source identity, version, rights/access status, local hash, and page map;
- equation and variable definitions with units;
- empirical inputs versus theoretical or project-derived terms;
- geometry, transport, boundary, scale, and steady/transient assumptions;
- predicted sign, scale, crossover, or trajectory feature;
- observationally equivalent alternatives and parameters that Phase 8 cannot identify;
- the cheapest Phase 8 discriminator and the required measurement adapter; and
- a pre-run result that would weaken or reject the hypothesis.

Use primary papers for load-bearing equations. Reviews and monographs may route discovery and
explain context, but they do not silently replace the cited derivation. Preserve disagreements
instead of synthesizing one preferred law prematurely.

## Steps

- [ ] **K0 — Register this bounded research contract.** Commit this plan and the live-state pointer
  before further external discovery.
- [ ] **K1 — Adjudicate the OpenAlex lead and existing holdings.** Resolve the 1987 Wang paper,
  inspect the current 2026 Harrington–Pokrifka source family, and inventory already-held theory
  papers so no source is downloaded twice.
- [ ] **K2 — Run the bounded currency/gap pass.** Execute at most the three query families above,
  plus exact-record/citation lookups justified by a retained source. Record request counts,
  screening decisions, and the stopping reason. Archive only sources that change a theory capsule.
- [ ] **K3 — Extract and reproduce core theory.** Build capsules for facet kinetics, diffusion and
  thermal resistance, pressure-conditioned kinetics, forcing/history, ventilation, initiation
  state, latent heat, curvature, step-source/hollowing, and printed alternative kinetics.
- [ ] **K4 — Calculate discriminators.** Reproduce source examples or limiting cases, calculate
  dimensionless regimes and expected effect sizes under the exact Phase 8 conditions, and label
  every result by derivation status and uncertainty.
- [ ] **K5 — Publish the knowledge baseline.** Write `research/phase9-knowledge-sources.md`, the
  structured files under `evidence/phase9-knowledge-baseline-v1/`, and the plain-English
  `docs/phase9-knowledge-guide.md`. Update the proposed consumer plan only where the evidence
  actually changes an arm or its order.
- [ ] **K6 — Audit and close the preparation.** Give the interpretation a skeptical non-author
  pass, repair blockers, verify links/hashes/calculation replay, run the required exact test suite,
  and leave a concrete adoption decision for the maker.

## Out of scope

- Solver, runner, app, GPU, parameter-table, or checkpoint changes.
- Executing or scoring a Phase 9 arm.
- A new held-out split, validation label, or claim that Phase 8 observations prove a mechanism.
- Bulk processing the 470-item Phase 8 metadata/full-text backlog.
- Downloading every citing or related paper, scraping publisher sites, parallel same-host requests,
  or automatic retry loops.
- Inventing numeric targets from qualitative diagrams or inaccessible abstracts.
- Choosing a final mechanism before competing predictions are written down.

## Tried and rejected

- **Treat the title's “new model” as evidence of a recent paper.** Rejected: captured OpenAlex
  metadata dates Wang Angsheng's paper to 1987. Its scientific value must come from the model and
  later lineage, not the adjective in its title.
- **Restart broad Phase 8 discovery for Phase 9.** Rejected: Phase 8 already closed its measurement
  gaps. Phase 9 needs mechanism-bearing sources, so search is limited to named theory gaps and
  source currency.
- **Make one summary per paper.** Rejected: paper summaries create reading volume without a usable
  experiment contract. Capsules are organized by hypotheses and discriminating predictions.
- **Require a new proof framework before reading theory.** Rejected as disproportionate. Page
  locators, hashes, replayable calculations, a bounded search record, and one skeptical audit cover
  the real correctness and provenance risks.

## Open questions

- Whether the 1987 Wang paper is obtainable from an authorized full-text source and whether its
  model is distinct from the later resistance/shape-function lineage.
- Whether the 2026 step-source theory changes the existing module shelf or becomes a sub-hypothesis
  inside direct facet kinetics and forcing/history rather than a separate implementation arm.
- Which calculations materially separate models under the available Phase 8 conditions; equations
  that yield observationally equivalent predictions should be marked non-identifiable, not promoted.
