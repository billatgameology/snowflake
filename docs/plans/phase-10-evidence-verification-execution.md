Create new versioned overlays rather than editing either Phase 8 book. Every 8A entry and 8B record
gets independent fields for phenomenon class, charter model-class scope and cited reason, zero or

ownership, and current decision eligibility. Mixed and unresolved remain first-class. A currently
missing mapping, operator, boundary, or numerical qualification does not turn an otherwise
in-scope phenomenon into permanent model-class exclusion.

### A-I — post-freeze intake and finite currency

and Phase 10 eligibility before B consumes them. Reuse existing custody and source work rather than
re-performing it. Run one closed currency snapshot for the source lineages B actually selects,

version change to a consumed source, a newly selected load-bearing family, an unavailable priority
lead becoming available, or a maker-scheduled refresh. A-I is a structurally verified static
packet: it does not open or acquire new source bytes. The shared six-target direct acquisition and
any resulting private-NAS publication belong to B's separately launch-governed `b-acquisition`
packet after A-I closes.

### B — finite observation and apparatus bridges

Run all six required dispositions without skipping the cross-pressure branch:

1. **B1a — Sei–Gonda planar facets:** source-specific apparatus-to-surface forcing, current
   Princeton-edition comparison, and uncertainty or a predeclared non-statistical sensitivity
   envelope.
2. **B1b — supported pressure/residual apparatus:** local vapor and temperature forcing, support
   heat path, asymmetric transfer, evolving geometry, and matched cross-pressure observation.
3. **B2 — controlled-velocity ventilation:** a source-complete controlled-velocity dataset or an
   operand-level refusal.
4. **B3 — same-geometry cross-pressure separation:** an eligible one-factor/crossed design or a
   terminal non-identification finding after the registered source and temperature-conflict work.
5. **B4 — HP26 semantics:** separately bind the final theory article and the measurement Methods;
   neither is allowed to stand in for the other.
6. **B5 — calibrated surface-state observables:** freeze the finite media/feature roster and
   calibration/operator/uncertainty rules before numeric inspection, then publish every attempted
   extraction or refusal.

Freeze each reference, expected result, norm, tolerance, roster, and negative control in a commit
before implementing its production comparison:

1. the existing analytic constant-coefficient radial control at 0.7, 0.35, 0.175, and 0.0875 µm,

3. a small symmetric moving-interface first-event control with independently computable event
   time, tied attaching orbit, topology, post-event field, and kinetic ledger, plus a mutation that
   changes event timing or topology.

sharing the production operator, that layer records a refusal. C0V is PASS only when all three
layers and their negative controls pass; a fail/refusal remains a terminal diagnostic result and
blocks any later C5 claim.
