## Goal

Determine which existing laboratory records can be mapped honestly to this model, which missing
apparatus or observation semantics make a comparison impossible, and whether three independent
numerical references support the machinery that a later target-specific comparison would use.
This is a bounded development-evidence package. It improves scope, source, observation, and
numerical foundations without running a scientific habit row, changing surface physics, or
claiming validation.

The maker selected the candidate plan's recommended package: **A-S + A-I + B + C0 + C0V with
packet-specific A-P**. The separate early numerical-risk probe and every target-facing package
remain unselected.

## Package contract

### A-S — scope and authority overlay

Create new versioned overlays rather than editing either Phase 8 book. Every 8A entry and 8B record
gets independent fields for phenomenon class, charter model-class scope and cited reason, zero or
more representability blockers, specimen/apparatus compatibility, immutable evidence role, phase
ownership, and current decision eligibility. Mixed and unresolved remain first-class. A currently
missing mapping, operator, boundary, or numerical qualification does not turn an otherwise
in-scope phenomenon into permanent model-class exclusion.

### A-I — post-freeze intake and finite currency

Disposition all 14 retained payloads for identity/version, rights, lineage, duplicates, purpose,
and Phase 10 eligibility before B consumes them. Reuse existing custody and source work rather than
re-performing it. Run one closed currency snapshot for the source lineages B actually selects,
with exact queries, dates, endpoints, and terminal outcomes. Reopen only for a correction or
version change to a consumed source, a newly selected load-bearing family, an unavailable priority
lead becoming available, or a maker-scheduled refresh.

### B — finite observation and apparatus bridges

Run all six required dispositions without skipping the cross-pressure branch:

1. **B1a — Sei–Gonda planar facets:** source-specific apparatus-to-surface forcing, current
   Princeton-edition comparison, and uncertainty or a predeclared non-statistical sensitivity
   envelope.
2. **B1b — supported pressure/residual apparatus:** local vapor and temperature forcing, support
   heat path, asymmetric transfer, evolving geometry, and matched cross-pressure observation.
3. **B2 — controlled-velocity ventilation:** a source-complete controlled-velocity dataset or an
   operand-level refusal.
4. **B3 — same-geometry cross-pressure separation:** an eligible one-factor/crossed design or a
   terminal non-identification finding after the registered source and temperature-conflict work.
5. **B4 — HP26 semantics:** separately bind the final theory article and the measurement Methods;
   neither is allowed to stand in for the other.
6. **B5 — calibrated surface-state observables:** freeze the finite media/feature roster and
   calibration/operator/uncertainty rules before numeric inspection, then publish every attempted
   extraction or refusal.

B starts with A-I's retained tranche. It permits one named acquisition round for the complete HP26
article, Pokrifka–Moyle–Harrington 2025 Methods, Keller–Hallett 1982, the complete current Princeton
monograph, Zhao's main article, and Zhao's identified S2 companion video. Each still-missing
operand permits at most one separately committed targeted search packet with no more than three
services/endpoints, four queries, and 25 unique candidates after deduplication, plus a frozen date
window and terminal dispositions. There is no broad discovery or recursive citation chase.
Purchase, provider contact, experiment execution, and credential use are not authorized.

B may return exact candidate scope and budget for E, F, or H. It may not implement E or H, write or
send an F request, or authorize any of them automatically.

### C0 — existing-byte numerical diagnostic

Independently parse the committed Phase 6 ladder artifact, reproduce its registered comparisons,
and rank only persisted observable/error-source pairs. C0 may use attached count, aspect ratio,
final extent, symmetry error, cycles, sweeps, timing/RSS, stop reason, and registered inputs. It
must explicitly refuse occupancy, checkpoints, crystallographic spans, trajectories, or any
quantity not present or independently derivable. The all-neutral habit rows do not establish a
robust habit observable.

### C0V — absolute numerical verification

Freeze each reference, expected result, norm, tolerance, roster, and negative control in a commit
before implementing its production comparison:

1. the existing analytic constant-coefficient radial control at 0.7, 0.35, 0.175, and 0.0875 µm,
   including the uniform-field and shell identities and an independently derived growth velocity;
2. a static three-dimensional aggregate-v6/monopole-matched field and flux control over at least
   three grid levels, with volume-weighted L2 and L-infinity norms, expected order, convergence and
   drift bounds, and a boundary-stencil mutation; and
3. a small symmetric moving-interface first-event control with independently computable event
   time, tied attaching orbit, topology, post-event field, and kinetic ledger, plus a mutation that
   changes event timing or topology.

Self-convergence is not an accuracy reference. If the static reference cannot be derived without
sharing the production operator, that layer records a refusal. C0V is PASS only when all three
layers and their negative controls pass; a fail/refusal remains a terminal diagnostic result and
blocks any later C5 claim.

### A-P — packet-specific obligation preflight

Before any C0/C0V producer or publication packet runs, freeze a matrix mapping every registered
output/check to its producer, artifact path or field, independent evaluator where required, and
proportionate negative control. Preflight refuses an output with no production path, a check with
no caller, a dirty or disallowed head, wrong runtime, duplicate/missing roster item, unsafe shared
path, insufficient disk, or stale writer. Its adversarial fixtures must remove one producer and
make one check uncalled. A-P establishes obligation completeness, not scientific correctness.

Routine A-S/A-I/B source reading does not pretend to be a scientific launch and is not blocked on
machine-only C0V launch checks. Any B protocol that registers outputs or checks still enters the
package-level obligation matrix before its deciding extraction.

  execute at process concurrency one so host contention cannot contaminate their cost record.

  not consume the scientific C0V process-hour budget. They still use unique logs when backgrounded.
- Recheck runtime, memory, disk, governed-NAS availability, and stale project processes at each
  executable launch. A prior readiness observation is context, not inherited proof.
