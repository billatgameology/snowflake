# Plan — Living scroll documentary through Phase 10

- **Phase:** Maker-directed Journey/media exploration, parallel to the scientific roadmap
- **Status:** in progress
- **Started:** 2026-08-15
- **Last touched:** 2026-08-15 by OpenAI Codex

## Goal

Record and integrate the maker's direction that the Snow Crystal Journey will not end with Phase 6:
scientific work is intended to continue through Phase 10 after Phase 6 closes, while media creation
proceeds in parallel. Shape the proposed whole-project documentary and manga-inspired vertical
website as one versioned, chaptered story system: reader-controlled scrolling, narrated playback,
and deterministic video export are separate performances of the same approved narrative score.

This is a media and roadmap-intent record. It does not silently accept the currently proposed,
uncharted Phase 8–10 drafts, change their dependencies, begin their scientific work, reopen the
frozen education site, or alter Phase 6's active implementation plan.

## Done when

There is no charter milestone for this media work. This planning unit is done when:

- the maker's two new directions are preserved verbatim in the append-only Journey source
  transcript;
- the Journey overview says the documentary is a living, chaptered synthesis continuing through
  the intended Phase 10 horizon and that media production is a parallel workstream;
- the media and numbering specifications define one versioned narrative score, distinct scroll,
  narrated-playback, and deterministic-export modes, source/correction bindings, and outlet-native
  derivatives without restoring a universal finished master;
- the social strategy names the documentary as a rich source reservoir while retaining separate
  hook, payoff, framing, and fit review for selected derivatives;
- the existing Chapter 1 long-form package is recorded as the bounded pilot input, not silently
  expanded to Chapters 2–10;
- `docs/PROGRESS.md` accurately records the direction, authority limits, and next media step; and
- a skeptical read-only review finds no conflict with the active Phase 6 plan, education freeze,
  proposed Phase 8–10 status, Journey chronology, or outlet-native policy, followed by
  `git diff --check` and `node scripts/lint-rule7.mjs`.

## Approach

Keep the existing `SCJ` chronology as the factual and corrective source spine. Add a documentary
synthesis layer above it:

```text
Journey entries + approved source packages
                    |
          versioned narrative score
          /          |             \
  scroll/read   watch/listen   fixed-frame export
      page          page          documentary
                                      |
                         separately briefed derivatives
```

The score is the shared authored object, not the exported movie. Its chapter and scene records bind
narration, prose, visual state, sources, scientific-status wording, correction state, timing, and
capture inputs. Natural scrolling remains reader-paced; narrated playback begins intentionally and
is time-driven; export is fixed-frame and deterministic. The documentary may be feature-length but
remains modular, addressable, resumable, and versioned by a named scientific/story horizon.

The first named horizon is the programme through Phase 10, not a claim that the film is complete at
Phase 6. Phase 7 is chartered; Phases 8–10 remain proposed until their required charter amendment
and ADR work. Media may be planned and produced alongside science, but publication, scientific
claims, and education-site edits retain their existing gates.

## Out of scope

- Building the scroll website, story runtime, capture renderer, editor, or publication pipeline.
- Editing `docs/education/**` before the Phase 6 freeze lifts.
- Running or changing any solver, evidence campaign, gate, or scientific protocol.
- Accepting or beginning proposed Phases 8–10; that requires the charter and ADR process named in
  their plans.
- Publishing, uploading, assigning a public `SCJ` identity, or treating this planning record as
  audience evidence.
- Assuming that a documentary excerpt automatically fits a short-feed destination.

## Tried and rejected

- **Make the final long-video bytes the universal master.** Rejected because corrections,
  responsive layouts, accessibility, deterministic rerenders, and outlet-native hooks need the
  deeper narrative score and reusable source packages.
- **Stop the documentary at Phase 6.** Rejected by the maker's 2026-08-15 direction; Phase 6 is a
  major act and scientific result, not the end of the programme or its media story.
- **Replace the gapless Journey with one endless page.** Rejected because the existing entries,
  stable routes, correction graph, and source chronology remain the documentary's reconstructable
  foundation.
- **Let scroll position trigger uncontrolled voiceover fragments.** Rejected because reader pace
  and spoken time are independent. Reader mode and narrated playback need distinct controllers.
- **Treat parallel media production as permission to reopen frozen education implementation.**
  Rejected. Media production is a separate workstream; the current education-site freeze remains
  until Phase 6 closes unless the maker separately changes that boundary.
