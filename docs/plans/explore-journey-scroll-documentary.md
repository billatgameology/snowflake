## Approach

Keep the existing `SCJ` chronology as the factual and corrective source spine. Add a documentary
synthesis layer above it:

```text
Journey entries + approved source packages
                    |
          versioned narrative score
          /          |             \
  scroll/read   watch/listen   fixed-frame export
      page          page          documentary
                                      |
                         separately briefed derivatives
```

The score is the shared authored object, not the exported movie. Its chapter and scene records bind
narration, prose, visual state, sources, scientific-status wording, correction state, timing, and
capture inputs. Natural scrolling remains reader-paced; narrated playback begins intentionally and
is time-driven; export is fixed-frame and deterministic. The documentary may be feature-length but
remains modular, addressable, resumable, and versioned by a named scientific/story horizon.

The first named horizon is the programme through Phase 10, not a claim that the film is complete at
Phase 6. The scientific programme has already advanced non-sequentially: Phases 8 and 9 are complete,
Phase 7 is independently eligible but unstarted, Phase 6 remains active, and Phase 10 remains
uncharted and inactive. Media may be planned and produced alongside science, but publication,
scientific claims, and education-site edits retain their existing gates.

## Tried and rejected

- **Make the final long-video bytes the universal master.** Rejected because corrections,
  responsive layouts, accessibility, deterministic rerenders, and outlet-native hooks need the
  deeper narrative score and reusable source packages.
- **Stop the documentary at Phase 6.** Rejected by the maker's 2026-08-15 direction; Phase 6 is a
  major act with a published measured-only negative result while its gate remains active, not the
  end of the programme or its media story.
- **Replace the gapless Journey with one endless page.** Rejected because the existing entries,
  stable routes, correction graph, and source chronology remain the documentary's reconstructable
  foundation.
- **Let scroll position trigger uncontrolled voiceover fragments.** Rejected because reader pace
  and spoken time are independent. Reader mode and narrated playback need distinct controllers.
- **Treat parallel media production as permission to reopen frozen education implementation.**
  Rejected. Media production is a separate workstream; the current education-site freeze remains
  until Phase 6 closes unless the maker separately changes that boundary.
