# Plan — Snow Crystal Journey media content specification

- **Phase:** Maker-directed exploration, outside Phase 6 scope
- **Status:** in progress
- **Started:** 2026-08-09
- **Last touched:** 2026-08-09 by OpenAI Codex

## Goal

Define a practical, sustainable media specification for the Snow Crystal Journey. The specification
must separate durable house choices from mutable platform limits and make it possible to produce one
high-quality content artifact without forcing every daily entry into every medium.

## Done when

`docs/journey/MEDIA-SPEC.md` records:

- the default short-video duration bands and when each is appropriate;
- voiceover pace and word-budget guidance;
- on-screen text, caption, title, description, and call-to-action rules;
- aspect ratio, resolution, frame-rate, safe-area, subtitle, thumbnail, and accessibility defaults;
- a clear music, ambience, sound-effect, and rights policy;
- provenance/disclosure rules for photographs, model output, diagrams, and generated illustration;
- master/archive deliverables and platform-specific adaptations for every retained channel in
  `docs/journey/SOCIAL-MEDIA.md`;
- dated, direct sources for mutable platform limits, with unknown or account-dependent limits named
  rather than guessed; and
- a lightweight pre-publication checklist suitable for daily use.

## Approach

Use one conservative house master as the reusable source, then define platform overrides only where
the destination requires or materially benefits from them. Treat platform maxima as compatibility
ceilings, not creative targets. Prefer official platform documentation; when a platform does not
publish a dependable limit, say so and require a preflight check in the actual account interface.

Editorial rules remain project choices and are labeled as such. Platform constraints carry a source
and verification date. Music and third-party media are permitted only through an explicit rights
path that survives cross-platform publication; no platform library license is presumed to transfer
to another destination.

## Out of scope

- Producing, recording, publishing, scheduling, or uploading any content.
- Opening or configuring social-media accounts.
- Editing `docs/education/**`, solver code, evidence, the charter, or Phase 6 records.
- Choosing channel branding, account names, monetization, advertising, or analytics targets.
- Treating this exploratory specification as a scientific evidence protocol.

## Tried and rejected

- **Use each platform's maximum duration as the content target.** Rejected: maxima change and do not
  describe what makes a daily Journey entry sustainable or effective.
- **Require one identical upload everywhere.** Rejected: one clean master should be reusable, while
  titles, captions, crops, localization, and context remain native to the destination.
- **Use music by default.** Rejected: narration and scientific visuals are load-bearing; music must
  earn its place and must have a cross-platform rights path.
- **Copy music from a platform library into the master.** Rejected: an in-platform license is not
  presumed to authorize redistribution elsewhere.

## Open questions

- Whether the maker wants a spoken or visual recurring Journey signature once the first production
  trials establish that it does not consume too much of a 15-second piece.
- Whether WeChat / Weixin receives translated subtitles only or fully localized narration.
- Whether live sessions get their own specification after the first one is actually planned.
