## Editorial spine

One sentence, per the series bible guardrail (mechanism named before any asset is selected):
**A snow crystal is built molecule-by-molecule out of vapour in a one-way relay from supercooled
cloud droplets, so its existence, its fuel limit, and its symmetry all follow from where the
water came from — not from any design.**

Answer-at-60%: the vapour-pressure relay (the "one-way robbery") lands at 9:22–10:17, ≈66–72 %
of runtime, then the video turns to consequences (too cold to snow) and meaning (nobody designs

## Description (draft)

A snowflake is not frozen rain — frozen rain is sleet, and it looks nothing like a snowflake.
This is the first video in a series on where snow crystals actually come from: a factory in the
sky that builds solid ice straight out of gas, one molecule at a time, fuelled by about 100,000
liquid droplets per crystal — none of which ever touch the crystal as liquid. Along the way:
why nothing freezes at 0 °C, why "too cold to snow" is real, and why the six arms of a star
match without ever communicating.

Based on Kenneth Libbrecht's monograph *Snow Crystals* (arXiv:1910.06389). All animations in
this video are original diagrams; claims are cited to the printed edition in the pinned
fact-check comment.
