# 0050 — Adopt Phase 9 modular physics experiments

- **Date:** 2026-08-12
- **Status:** accepted by maker direction
- **Charter impact:** document marker/current-revision record, §1.5, §2.7, §3.2 sequencing,

## Context

Phase 8B completed a verified 51-record priority benchmark and an explicit residual source universe.
The subsequent Phase 9 knowledge baseline bound 18 mechanism sources and 15 testable hypotheses.
Those inputs make several cheap, discriminating development experiments possible before the
Phase 6 numerical ladder closes: measurement adapters, source and protocol reconciliation,
scalar bulk-transfer replay, direct-facet calculations, analytic transport bounds, and small
manufactured tests. None requires the unpublished Phase 6 verdict or the Windows evidence host.

The phase was nevertheless not authorized. Charter v1.26 made work sequential by default and said
Phases 9–10 remained uncharted. Creating branch `phase/9` and the isolated worktree did not change
that authority. On 2026-08-12 the maker directed, verbatim, “go ahead and do all of the work in
phase 9 that can be done,” and then added, “please remember the plan was created before phase 8
discovered many more papers and saved on the NAS, please be sure to include those.” This decision
adopts the phase and makes the second direction an execution prerequisite rather than treating the
older proposed plan as a frozen paper roster.

That source correction is already scientifically material. The registered Phase 8 source universe
contains cross-pressure and gas-switch histories, morphology matrices, growth–sublimation–regrowth
sequences, roughness trajectories, and categorical nucleation interventions that the proposed
consumer draft did not incorporate. They require a separate gas/pressure transport arm, an
observable surface-state rival, an electric-field compatibility field for levitation histories,
and a categorical initiation adapter. They do not by themselves isolate pressure-conditioned
surface kinetics, prove hidden memory, or turn any development comparison into validation.

Phase 6 remains active for a few more days. Its Windows host has shown severe contention during the
timing-sensitive numerical ladder. The relevant boundary is therefore artifact, claim, and compute
isolation—not an artificial dependency on Phase 6 completion.

   by [`phase-9-execution.md`](../plans/phase-9-execution.md). It may run concurrently with Phase 6.
   Source/protocol compatibility precedes any score; one-factor probes precede combinations; a
   source-block, ineligible comparison, no effect, disagreement, or all-no-pass result is a valid
   terminal outcome. `GGThreshold` and `LibbrechtKinetics` remain unchanged permanent controls.

   the registered Phase 8 local/acquired universe—28 acquired PDFs plus 23 local containers,
   of which 21 are PDFs and two are ZIPs—as well as the Phase 9 knowledge register and relevant

   reused. Before an affected arm freezes, check current versions, corrections, supplements, public

   and numeric inputs receive the targeted visual/numeric check required by decision 0049. The 51
   records are the score-ready priority benchmark, not an assertion that the remaining literature
   contains no relevant mechanism or protocol evidence.

4. **Correct the experimental shelf before scoring.** The initial order is D-BT; M-F plus M-K2;
   M-V; M-PT plus M-LH and the distinct M-GP gas/pressure transport arm; exact-path M-H plus the
   observable M-SR surface-state arm; residual M-PK; the M-W/M-SS/M-SR hollowing rivals; M-S; M-GT;
   then supported one-factor promotions and combinations. In particular:

   - D-BT records electrodynamic levitation and electric-field exposure as protocol fields. It may
     compare transfer rules within that apparatus, but it cannot claim non-training transfer unless
     the Lamb experiment-ID lineage join resolves every primary history as non-overlapping.
   - M-K2 is already transcribed and applies only to prism rows in its printed domain; the basal
     broad-facet rule remains a separate comparator.
   - M-V blocks absolute free-fall scoring until a quantitative controlled-velocity source or a
     pre-registered conservative eligibility bound is available. It does not block source
     extraction, directional interventions, or analytic transport calculations.
   - M-GP tests aggregate gas/pressure transport response. Existing cross-pressure/gas evidence
     does not isolate an M-PK surface-kinetics residual; that stronger promotion remains blocked
     until a compatible source or design separates the effects.
   - M-SR starts with observable roughness, pocket, lateral-facet, and regrowth features. Those
     observations may challenge M-W and M-SS without being converted into an invented hidden state.
   - M-S may use denominated Sato–Kikuchi categorical contrasts, while the two Bacon aggregates
     retain their missing-denominator and exposure-confounding limits.

5. **Freeze every deciding experiment before output.** Each arm records exact source bytes and
   locators, lineage and rights, represented and unrepresented protocol dimensions, transformations
   and uncertainty, baseline and intervention, pre-run expected effect and no-effect branch,
   type-appropriate ablation, control, or refusal, finite roster, stop/resource bound, numerical
   controls, weighting, and claim limits.
   Protocol incompatibility is a tagged result rather than an exception or an invented conversion.
   Raw observations remain separate from interpolation, fitting, differentiation, coalescing, and
   model output. Series length alone never determines cross-family weight.

6. **Use development evidence honestly.** Every Phase 8B row and Phase 9 output is development
   evidence. Source replay, fitted input, same-lineage comparison, training-overlap comparison,
   software ablation, and analytic bound retain those labels. Phase 9 cannot grant a
   quantitative-validation label, satisfy the Phase 6 or Phase 7 gate, or retroactively relabel a
   Phase 8 split. Future confirmation of a Phase-9-developed mechanism requires genuinely unseen
   observations frozen before their values are inspected.

7. **Keep concurrent work isolated.** Phase 9 uses branch `phase/9` and worktree
   `/Users/clipper/github/snowflake-phase-9`. It writes new `evidence/phase9-*` bundles and new
   Phase 9 NAS roots. Phase 6's Windows worktree, processes, plans, artifacts, and unpublished
   verdict are read-only. No Phase 9 process uses that evidence host during a timing-sensitive row.
   While it remains occupied, the Mac tranche may perform S0/S1/S3, source acquisition/extraction,
   analytic and planar probes, and small manufactured tests. Solver-heavy three-dimensional work
   waits for host release or a separately frozen Mac resource probe.

8. **Resolve known no-pass branches explicitly.** Substrate-supported measurements are ineligible
   for unsupported absolute transfer unless support is represented or bounded; full free-fall
   scoring remains M-V-gated; the exact M-SS implementation remains source-blocked without the full
   governing equations; M-W has no physical score until its width mapping and scale are frozen;
   M-H cannot earn a hidden-memory claim without a matched state-resolving comparison; M-PK cannot
   earn a surface-residual claim from jointly varying gas/transport experiments; M-GT remains a
   numerical diagnostic without a matched curvature intervention. No combination campaign is
   authorized until at least one arm passes its frozen one-factor discriminator.

9. **Apply proportionate assurance once.** Executable Phase 9 changes run exact `npm test`.
   Published quantitative evidence is re-derived from its bytes by a component that did not inherit
   the producer's verdict and includes named negative controls for its fail-open boundaries. This
   adoption receives one targeted governance audit; reviews do not recurse.

10. **Adopt the charter Done-when unchanged in the execution plan.** Phase 9 is complete only under
    the byte-identical Done-when paragraph quoted below. An all-no-pass phase is complete; changing
    the criteria after seeing results is not.

gas/pressure outcomes directly to surface kinetics; inventing missing ventilation, width, memory,

## Alternatives considered

**Wait for Phase 6 to close.** Rejected because the executable tranche consumes completed Phase 8
and knowledge artifacts, not the ladder verdict. Isolation handles the real risk.

**Run the older proposed plan unchanged.** Rejected because the final NAS corpus contains
decision-changing experiments and rivals that the draft predates.

**Start with a three-dimensional arm.** Rejected because scalar transfer, direct-facet, analytic
transport, and adapter failures are cheaper discriminators, and the Windows host is occupied.

**Preserve the Phase 8A held-out split.** Rejected because decision 0048's successor has zero

**Treat every registered source as a numeric benchmark.** Rejected because qualitative/context
constraints and protocol refusals are scientifically useful without inventing coordinate rows.
