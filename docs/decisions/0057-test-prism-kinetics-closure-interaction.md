## Context and decision

The completed longer comparisons isolate a persistent basal-side cavity effect, while prism-only
kinetics and ordinary no-dip kinetics with geometric completion disabled produce resealing or
shallow transient pits. Those single interventions do not determine their interaction: removing
completion throughout growth changes the subsequent geometry and diffusion field.

Permit exactly the combined opt-ins `experimentalFacetDips: "prism-only"` and
`experimentalHoleFilling: "disabled"` under the separate identity
`post-phase10-prism-holefill-interaction-v1`. Keep M1 base metadata, aggregate-v6 and constant
environment; preserve the existing prepared coefficient, coupled Robin/fill evaluation and
disabled-completion path without altering their numerical equations. Ordinary checkpoint export
and timeline changes remain unsupported. All other simultaneous opt-in combinations remain
outside this finite experiment.

The active discovery plan registers two new larger-domain rows and six reused controls before
implementation. They form prism dip absent/present by completion enabled/disabled comparisons,
with the basal dip absent throughout. The readout must distinguish persistent multi-plane open
cavities from recurrent surface pits or sealed vacancies. This is internal-model development,
not a fitted physical law, width-dependent SDAK, grid convergence or validation.
