# 0062 — Continue Phase 10 exploration toward multiple promising leads

- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Status:** accepted by explicit maker direction: “update phase 10, we are going to keep exploring until we have multiple promising leads.”
- **Charter impact:** document marker/current-revision record, §2.5's facet and discovery-resume phase descriptions, and §3.2's Phase 10 scope and completion criterion updated in this session. Scientific experiment contracts, executed results and validation authority are unchanged.

## Context

The August verification package closed negative under decision 0054. Subsequent adaptive discovery
explored mechanisms, numerical sensitivity and growth histories under separately accepted decisions
0055–0060 and finite campaign plans. The maker now identifies this continuing exploration as Phase 10
and wants multiple promising leads before closing it. Treating one completed package as closure of
that broader purpose makes the live phase index misleading.

This is a phase-governance amendment, not a result reinterpretation. No new solver run, source
search, experimental operator or scientific pass is created here.

## Decision

1. Keep **Phase 10 active** as the exploration umbrella for the existing portfolio and its finite
   follow-ups. No Phase 11 starts now. The August package is a completed negative milestone within
   the phase; its original frozen scope and Done-when remain applicable to that package only.
2. Use the following current exploration endpoint verbatim in the charter and active plan:

> Done when the maker accepts multiple distinct promising model-development leads, each supported by a named matched comparison, a result with its scope and uncertainty, the numerical, representation and observation checks relevant to that result (or a specifically recorded unresolved check), and a concrete next discriminating experiment. Repeated configurations of one effect do not count as separate leads. Current candidates do not close the phase automatically; the maker's portfolio review decides when enough leads are promising. This criterion creates no automatic gate or quantitative-validation label.

3. Preserve the closed August package's gate10 meaning, B refusal, C0 NO-PASS, C0V incomplete/non-PASS
   and zero S6 credit. Do not restore the retired executor, delete its retained locks, rerun refused
   attempts, or convert infrastructure stops into scientific dispositions.
4. Include the work previously called post-Phase-10 in the Phase 10 umbrella. Supersede only the
   phase-level naming/closure assertions in decisions 0054–0060; their historical bytes, outcomes,
   finite scientific permissions, isolation, checkpoint identities and claim limits remain intact.
   The two current §2.5 descriptions now refer specifically to the completed August verification
   package when saying it is not reopened. Legacy paths remain stable for provenance.
5. Continue through finite question-driven waves in the existing adaptive discovery and affected
   task plans. This is not blanket authorization for a new intervention or source/provider work;
   governing plan/ADR requirements still apply. Product, GPU parity and held-out validation remain
   Phase 7's separate scope and maker hold. Development evidence earns no physical identification
   or quantitative-validation label by being promising.

## Consequences

The live status matches the maker's continuing research purpose, while failed or incomplete
milestones retain their useful meaning. Multiple distinct leads encourage breadth beyond one warm
cavity effect. A promising lead can still carry an explicitly unresolved numerical qualification;
its next experiment must address the uncertainty rather than silently upgrading it.

Completion now requires a maker portfolio judgment. There is deliberately no automatic count-based
phase gate, calendar deadline or fixed number of runs. Historical post-Phase-10 filenames persist
and need their current-scope note so readers do not mistake legacy naming for live phase ownership.

## Alternatives considered

- **Start Phase 11 now:** adds a phase boundary without changing the exploration objective; the
  maker chose to continue Phase 10.
- **Leave Phase 10 complete and call everything post-phase:** conflicts with the maker's stated
  scope and stopping intent.
- **Reopen the August S6 recovery ladder:** changes neither the requested purpose nor the promising
  lead portfolio and contradicts decision 0054's retained termination.
- **Close automatically because several candidates are listed:** candidates still have unresolved
  questions; a list does not replace the maker's evidence-based portfolio review.
