  count.

- A public viewer sees only the simple Journey identity and canonical navigation.

  append-only events.

## What the maker actually handles

Before the pipeline exists, this volume requires the manual interim publication manifest defined in

## Sample-round findings

1. The public `SCJ` number stays simple even when the archive becomes detailed.
2. Publication suffixes must remain scoped to an entry; platform-specific numbering would fail as
   soon as the channel mix changes on Tuesday.
3. Release manifests are useful for accessibility and localization, not merely media encoding.
4. Event history matters most on failure, fan-out, editing, correction, and live-archive days.
5. A material correction can preserve a useful earlier artifact without preserving its outdated
   interpretation as current.
6. Covering every retained channel in one five-entry week is operationally possible on paper but is
   intentionally not the default cadence.
7. The next sample round should remove most destinations and test whether the scheme still feels
   natural during an ordinary, low-output week.
