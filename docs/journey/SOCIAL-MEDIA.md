# Social media coverage

**Status:** exploratory channel plan for the Snow Crystal Journey. This file does not authorize
publishing or automated posting.

The Journey website is the permanent chronological home. Social platforms do different editorial
jobs around that record. A day may suit one outlet or content family without being posted
everywhere.

The strategy is **one real Journey event, one reusable source package, and separately briefed
outlet editions**. Research, code, clean captures, renders, editable diagrams, photographs, voice,
transcripts, and provenance may be shared. A finished edit, opening, script, caption, card order, or
call to action is not presumed to fit a second destination.

The required 15–59 second watchable serial edition belongs to the owned Journey page and its
watch-in-order sequence. It is not the universal social master. A selected short-feed edition may
also serve as the serial edition when it genuinely satisfies both jobs.

The production rules, including hook integrity, long-form structure, the default **no-music**
decision, accessibility, provenance labels, rights, and dated platform limits, live in the
[media specification](MEDIA-SPEC.md).

Every outward edition carries its public Journey identity and points to the canonical numbered
entry. The [numbering and reconstruction specification](NUMBERING.md) keeps one story sequence while
recording each outlet-specific artifact, release, destination placement, edit, failure, correction,
and removal separately.

## Editorial families

### Hook-first short discovery

TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, Snapchat Spotlight, and Weixin Channels all
need an immediate reason to watch, but they do not automatically receive the same finished video.
Each selected edition gets its own brief covering the first frame and first sentence, pacing,
on-screen copy, cover, caption, safe area, payoff, and destination action.

The hook may be a visible anomaly, transformation, failure, question, contrast, or extraordinary
crystal view. It must honestly represent the entry and deliver the promised movement or result in
the same piece. A series number or logo does not substitute for the hook, and a manufactured
cliffhanger does not substitute for a payoff.

Source footage and a clean body sequence may be reused. Identical finished bytes are reused only
after an explicit native-fit review finds that the same opening, pacing, labels, framing, and payoff
work for both destinations. That is an allowed outcome, not the production default.

### Long explanation and synthesis

YouTube is the primary searchable home for 3–8 minute recaps and 8–20 minute explainers or chapters.
Selected Facebook placements may receive a native longer explainer, human-centered project story,
or group-specific edition when that audience and context warrant it. The website carries the full
entry or synthesis and can embed the approved video.

Long-form needs a truthful title/thumbnail promise and a strong opening that promptly establishes
the question, stakes, and visual destination. It does not use the compressed two-second grammar of
a feed short, and a short is never padded into a chapter.

The living scroll documentary is the programme's richest long-horizon synthesis. It is intended to
continue through Phase 10 rather than treating Phase 6 as the finale, and its chapters, scenes,
visuals, narration, transcript, and clean assets may be produced while the scientific programme is
still advancing. Each documentary release names the exact horizon it actually covers; Phase 7 is
independently eligible but unstarted, Phases 8 and 9 are chartered and complete, Phase 6 remains
active, and Phase 10 is uncharted and inactive.

That documentary is a source reservoir, not a universal social edit. A selected chapter, scene, or
time range becomes an outlet edition only after a brief supplies the destination's honest opening,
context, payoff, aspect composition, scientific qualifier, accessibility treatment, and action.
The long-form conclusion may depend on setup or caveats outside an excerpt, so a derivative records
its exact source scenes and restores every qualifier needed to stand alone.

### Visual sequencing and evergreen discovery

Instagram carousels explain one idea through a deliberate card sequence. Pinterest receives a
searchable evergreen image, diagram, educational card, or concise video designed around long-lived
visual usefulness. A resize alone is not an editorial adaptation: card order, crop, headline,
context, and link purpose are checked for the selected outlet.

Stories are reminders and behind-the-scenes fragments. They never hold the only copy of evidence,
a correction, an artifact, or a useful link.

### Conversation and real-time notes

Threads receives a conversational observation, question, correction, or reflection. X receives the
compact result, failure, uncertainty, metric, or build status first. They may share the same fact
and approved visual, but not presumptively the same sentence or tone.

### Human localization

Weixin Channels, Official Accounts, and Moments are three editorial surfaces, not one translated
upload. Localization starts from an immutable approved source release and includes human authorship,
terminology and scientific review, cultural framing, accessibility, and manual publication. No
unattended, bulk, or continuous posting is authorized.

### Live, community, and direct follow

Twitch and YouTube Live use an event-specific run-of-show, moderation, caption, music-rights,
archive, and failure plan. A raw stream is source material, not automatically a finished chapter.

Discord, WhatsApp, and Telegram serve people who have opted into an existing relationship; they do
not inherit cold-feed urgency, public hashtags, or high-frequency promotional copy. Email and RSS
provide the reliable digest and correction path independent of social algorithms.

## Reusable source package

An entry's shared package may contain:

- the approved question, movement, claim status, sources, and correction state;
- code/model/run identities, deterministic states, capture recipes, and clean screen recordings;
- editable diagrams, high-quality stills, renders, animations, and unwatermarked source footage;
- clean voice, ambience and effects stems when they exist, transcript, captions, and visual
  description; and
- rights, privacy, provenance, accessibility, localization, and maker-review records.

A documentary synthesis package may additionally carry the exact narrative-score version, stable
act/scene keys, source-entry and release bindings, page anchors, film timecodes, deterministic cue
records, and correction map. These make reuse traceable; they do not waive the outlet brief.

Each outlet edition names the exact source members it used. An editorially distinct script,
opening, selection, pacing, language, framing, meaning, or correction lifecycle receives its own
artifact and approved release. A destination-only crop, caption export, codec, or transcode may
remain a technical rendition of the same artifact, but its exact bytes and copy still receive their
own destination-publication record.


## Operating principles

- Do not require every entry to appear on every retained channel.
- Preserve the website as the canonical Journey record; social posts are distribution copies.
- Put the visible/searchable Journey identity on every copy and link its stable numbered page where
  the destination permits.
- Upload native clean media rather than reposting a watermarked download from another platform.
- Share approved source assets, not presumptively finished stories. Give every selected outlet its
  own opening, pacing, framing, copy, payoff, accessibility check, and exact publication record.
- Reuse an identical finished edition only after recording that it fits every selected destination;
  production convenience alone is not a fit review.
- Treat WeChat / Weixin localization, account operation, and publication review as real work, not an
  automatic duplicate upload.
- New work may create immediate publication candidates, but the maker approves outward publication.
