# Social media coverage

**Status:** exploratory channel plan for the Snow Crystal Journey. This file does not authorize
publishing or automated posting.

The Journey website is the permanent chronological home. Social platforms distribute individual
steps and point people back to that record. A day may suit one platform group without being posted
everywhere.

The production rules, including the default **no-music** decision, 15–59 second daily-video band,
text budgets, accessibility, provenance labels, rights, and dated platform limits, live in the
[media specification](MEDIA-SPEC.md).

Every outward copy carries its public Journey identity and points to the canonical numbered entry.
The [numbering and reconstruction specification](NUMBERING.md) keeps one story sequence while
recording each destination placement, edit, failure, correction, and removal separately.

## Retained channels

| Channel | Role in the Journey | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| **Journey website** | Owned source and permanent archive | Every Journey entry, links, corrections, demos, crystals, and later chapters |
| **YouTube** | Searchable video home | Shorts for daily steps; long-form chapters, documentaries, and occasional live sessions |
| **TikTok** | Short-form discovery | Native vertical daily discoveries, experiments, failures, and crystal reveals |
| **Instagram** | Visual identity and short-form discovery | Reels, crystal images, diagram carousels, and Stories |
| **Facebook** | Broad and older audience plus groups | Reels and selected posts, usually shared from Instagram with little extra production |
| **Threads** | Conversational field notes | Questions, brief discoveries, images, corrections, and discussion |
| **X** | Real-time research and development conversation | Research notes, build updates, experiments, clips, and project milestones |
| **WeChat / Weixin** | China-facing distribution and community | Channels for video, Official Accounts for longer updates, and selected Moments sharing; localization is a separate editorial step |
| **Pinterest** | Evergreen visual discovery | Batched crystal images, diagrams, educational cards, and links to Journey pages |
| **Snapchat Spotlight** | Additional young-audience discovery | Selected vertical clips when the material suits the platform |
| **Discord** | Community home rather than discovery | Add later, after a recurring audience exists |
| **Twitch / YouTube Live** | Occasional live participation | Building sessions, experiments, demonstrations, and long crystal runs |
| **WhatsApp / Telegram channels** | Direct update distribution | Add later for followers who want an opt-in stream of new Journey entries |
| **Email / RSS** | Owned direct distribution | A digest and reliable follow mechanism independent of social algorithms |

WeChat, also include Official Accounts, Moments, Search, Mini Programs, and Channels. Sources:

## Distribution packets

### Vertical video

Produce one clean 9:16 master, a reviewed captioned derivative, and caption sidecars without another
platform's watermark, then upload the appropriate rendition natively:

- YouTube Shorts
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook Reels
- WeChat / Weixin Channels
- Snapchat Spotlight when selected

### Conversation

Adapt the day's movement into a short native field note, with one useful visual when available:

- Threads
- X
- WeChat / Weixin Official Account or Moments when localized and appropriate

### Archive, community, and evergreen discovery

- Journey website for the permanent entry
- Email / RSS for the digest
- Pinterest for reusable visual material
- Discord, WhatsApp, and Telegram after a community exists
- Twitch or YouTube Live for occasional participatory sessions


## Operating principles

- Do not require every entry to appear on every retained channel.
- Preserve the website as the canonical Journey record; social posts are distribution copies.
- Put the visible/searchable Journey identity on every copy and link its stable numbered page where
  the destination permits.
- Upload native clean media rather than reposting a watermarked download from another platform.
- Adapt the caption and framing to the platform even when the underlying video is shared.
- Treat WeChat / Weixin localization, account operation, and publication review as real work, not an
  automatic duplicate upload.
- New work may create immediate publication candidates, but the maker approves outward publication.
