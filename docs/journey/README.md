# Snow Crystal Journey

**Status:** exploratory concept. Nothing here is published content, scientific evidence, or a
Phase 6 claim.

## The idea

Build a slow, year-long-or-longer public journey through snow crystals. The maker began knowing
almost nothing and moves the investigation forward consistently, usually through a piece lasting
about 15 seconds to one minute. Progress does not need to be linear: discoveries, experiments,
failed attempts, corrections, new crystals, and steps backward all belong in the story.

The Journey is the chronological spine connecting everything else. The day's primary work can take
its natural form; the Journey does not require every medium or destination. Each public entry is
committed with one lightweight watchable serial edition so someone can follow the numbered sequence
without a missing episode. That edition may be the primary video itself or a restrained derivative
of a note, still, audio recording, interactive, model, or other artifact.

The serial edition belongs to the owned watch-in-order Journey; it is not a universal social-media
master. An entry instead preserves one reusable source package—approved facts, code or model state,
clean captures, editable visuals, audio, transcript, provenance, rights, and correction state—and
branches only into the outlet-and-purpose editions selected for that movement. Short feeds receive
an honest immediate hook and delivered payoff. A YouTube or Facebook explainer receives the longer
structure its subject needs. Threads, X, Instagram, Weixin, Pinterest, and the other retained
outlets keep their own editorial jobs. Finished bytes are reused only after an explicit native-fit
review; shared source material alone does not make two publications the same story edit.

Public Journey numbers are consecutive. Drafts use temporary production identities and the
`JOURNEY-NEXT` placeholder; abandoned, private, and embargoed work consumes no `SCJ` number. The
next permanent identity, such as `SCJ-0042`, is assigned by the atomic release that publishes and
binds its canonical page and serial package. An immediate independent live check must pass before
any social distribution or later Journey commit. From `SCJ-0001` through the latest entry, every
number remains occupied and keeps its record and route binding to the extent permitted; it resolves
to its entry or a safe unavailable-entry notice whenever law, privacy, safety, and rights allow.
Platform copies, artifacts, and approved releases remain attached to that identity instead of
creating competing story sequences.

## A day's entry

Each entry needs only:

1. The question or problem being pursued.
2. What moved, changed, failed, or became clearer.
3. The day's primary artifact; the journal note itself can fill this role.
4. A watchable serial edition, which may be the primary artifact itself.
5. The question or direction that follows.

Its canonical page also supplies Previous/Next in the full Journey, earlier/later links in the same
topic, current-understanding and correction links, and every retained artifact or publication copy.

The primary artifact may be a short video, research note, diagram, animation, interactive demo,
crystal, build update, failed experiment, correction, reflection, or eventually a chapter. A day
does not need to produce all of these, and the serial edition is a watchable index rather than a
requirement for a second elaborate production.

## How the pieces connect

- A crystal remains a specimen in the crystal collection; its Journey entry explains when and why
  it was made and links to its viewer, images, or growth video.
- An interactive remains in the lab or education experience; its Journey entry records the question
  and the step it answered.
- A React-coded page, interactive, model viewer, or educational scene can also be the visual source
  for a guided website demonstration, scripted playback, direct-frame animation, hybrid video,
  still, or preview loop. The implementation method is not a scientific-status label: the web
  experience remains interactive, while each linear output states its capture method, retains its
  underlying provenance, and points back to its exact source release, state, and capture recipe.
- A video remains a video; its Journey entry provides its place in the larger sequence.
- A research correction preserves the original entry, marks the affected claim or artifact, and
  links forward to the correction instead of rewriting the historical journey. The whole entry is
  called superseded only when the correction actually replaces it as a whole.
- A chapter is a later synthesis of many entries, not the required starting unit for each day.

The same entry can later be discovered chronologically, through a topic thread, through its media
type, or as part of a chapter or larger story arc.

## Working principles

- One honest step is enough for a day.
- The daily format may change; the sequence supplies continuity.
- Every committed public number keeps its identity and ordinary recovery route; ordinary workflow
  never creates a private or abandoned hole, while a binding later-removal exception is recorded
  without reusing or renumbering the public series.
- Failures and corrections are first-class entries, not missing days.
- The Journey preserves what was believed at the time; a separate current-understanding view can
  present the latest corrected account.
- New work may generate an immediate publication candidate, but outward-facing claims still receive
  review and maker approval before publication.
- Rich days can produce several later pieces, but they do not raise the minimum burden for ordinary
  days.
- Reuse research and source assets across editions; choose the opening, pacing, framing, copy, and
  payoff for the selected outlet rather than assuming one finished piece fits every destination.

## Current boundary

This folder is only the home for the concept. It does not start a production system, alter the
education site, publish media, or change any scientific status. The active Phase 6 education freeze
remains in force.
