# Snow Crystal Journey

**Status:** exploratory concept. Nothing here is published content, scientific evidence, or a
Phase 6 claim.

## The idea

Build a slow, year-long-or-longer public journey through snow crystals. The maker began knowing
almost nothing and moves the investigation forward consistently, usually through a piece lasting
about 15 seconds to one minute. Progress does not need to be linear: discoveries, experiments,
failed attempts, corrections, new crystals, and steps backward all belong in the story.

The Journey is the chronological spine connecting everything else. It is not another medium that
must be produced in addition to the day's work. Each entry records what changed and links to the
content created that day in its natural home.

Each approved entry receives one permanent identity such as `SCJ-0042`, shown publicly as
**Journey 0042**. It is an entry number, not a day number. Platform copies, artifacts, and approved
releases remain attached to that identity instead of creating competing story sequences.

## A day's entry

Each entry needs only:

1. The question or problem being pursued.
2. What moved, changed, failed, or became clearer.
3. The day's artifact, if there is one.
4. The question or direction that follows.

Its canonical page also supplies Previous/Next in the full Journey, earlier/later links in the same
topic, current-understanding and correction links, and every retained artifact or publication copy.

The artifact may be a short video, research note, diagram, animation, interactive demo, crystal,
build update, failed experiment, correction, reflection, or eventually a chapter. A day does not
need to produce all of these.

## How the pieces connect

- A crystal remains a specimen in the crystal collection; its Journey entry explains when and why
  it was made and links to its viewer, images, or growth video.
- An interactive remains in the lab or education experience; its Journey entry records the question
  and the step it answered.
- A video remains a video; its Journey entry provides its place in the larger sequence.
- A research correction preserves the original entry, marks the affected claim or artifact, and
  links forward to the correction instead of rewriting the historical journey. The whole entry is
  called superseded only when the correction actually replaces it as a whole.
- A chapter is a later synthesis of many entries, not the required starting unit for each day.

The same entry can later be discovered chronologically, through a topic thread, through its media
type, or as part of a chapter or larger story arc.

## Working principles

- One honest step is enough for a day.
- The daily format may change; the sequence supplies continuity.
- Failures and corrections are first-class entries, not missing days.
- The Journey preserves what was believed at the time; a separate current-understanding view can
  present the latest corrected account.
- New work may generate an immediate publication candidate, but outward-facing claims still receive
  review and maker approval before publication.
- Rich days can produce several later pieces, but they do not raise the minimum burden for ordinary
  days.

## Current boundary

This folder is only the home for the concept. It does not start a production system, alter the
education site, publish media, or change any scientific status. The active Phase 6 education freeze
remains in force.
