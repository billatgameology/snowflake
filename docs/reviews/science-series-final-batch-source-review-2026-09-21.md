## Source currency repairs to carry into new content

- Chapter 13's statement that pure cubic ice has never been observed is obsolete. The primary
  [2020 preparation study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-14346-5) reports ice without
  stacking disorder; [2023 molecular-resolution imaging](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-05864-5)
  follows cubic ice growth at 102 K. Those laboratory results do not establish its abundance in
  atmospheric snow. Avoid the chapter's broader “never photographed a molecule at a time” claim.
- The 2026 step-source paper is published, not merely a supplement to a submitted manuscript.
  Its model comparisons are distinct from the older levitated mass histories. Do not turn the
  latter into a proven movie of a single-crystal surface developing facets.
- A source author's judgement that one proposal is the only viable explanation is an attributed
  assessment, not an exhaustive theorem. Likewise, funding anecdotes do not describe the entire
  ice or atmospheric-science research community.
- Preserve exact observation conditions. A morphology map omitting pressure, seed, time and
  history is a useful summary but is not the complete experimental input to a model test.
