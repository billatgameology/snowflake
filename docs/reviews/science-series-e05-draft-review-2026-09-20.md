## Scientific and continuity review

The hard passage spans E05-01/02: the same small disturbance is first controlled, then amplified
under a different qualitative surface-response condition. Both outcomes add ice. A fixed marker,
old-material color and original outline prevent the reduction in relative bump height from looking
like melting or erasure. The outer supply remains fixed. The second case stages two geometry/growth
increments before exposing the comparison. Net delivery paths are schematics, not molecule tracks,
wind, measured flux or a solved concentration field. Their changing weight indicates the qualitative
advantage; no magnitude or transport law is inferred from their width.

Primary monograph passages were read from the local extracted pages for printed pp. 81–85,
90–91 and 102–107; the corresponding references and Chapter 5/6 prose were inspected. The
[2002 primary dendrite paper](https://www.snowcrystals.com/electric/1-s2.0-S0022024801020899-main.pdf)
was opened online, including its normal-growth analysis, curvature term and attachment-controlled
selection assumptions. Official arXiv metadata was checked for the v2 monograph. The web tool
could not fetch the whole monograph PDF because of its size; the existing local extraction supplied
the quoted page context. No source image is redistributed.

The script deliberately narrows several stronger Chapter 6 formulations:

- A perturbation can amplify when stabilizing effects do not compensate; every molecular bump on
  actual ice does not necessarily become a branch. Surface energy and attachment response are
  retained in the tip explanation.
- Six corners provide a common route to six primary arms under similar conditions. Exact simultaneous
  onset, perfect sixfold detail and a unique reconstructed past are not claimed.
- A free dendrite's near-tip shape can persist approximately under steady, unconstrained conditions.
  The roughly one-micrometer radius near −15 °C remains a condition-dependent inference in the
  reader. It is not shown as a direct resolved measurement or universal minimum feature size.
- The pressure dependence of selected radius is identified as a prediction in the cited account,
  not promoted to a modern confirmed measurement.
- Directional preference helps organize growth; real tips can split. The no-preference cartoon
  illustrates a primary numerical comparison and is explicitly not a new solve.
- Sidebranches can begin from growth fluctuations, with a characteristic scale and possible
  external coordination; each does not require a dust particle or compulsory complete randomness.
- The limited fractal resemblance does not supply the physical growth mechanism. Habit and
  branching complexity remain distinct; E06 still owns its map and conditions.

The existing website library was inspected at `src/growth/recorded-models.json`. Its

## Remaining review limits and next production questions

The shapes, gas tint, path weights, rates, before-outlines and tip comparisons are original teaching
geometry. They cannot establish a physical branching threshold, crystal size, tip radius, speed or
mass budget. The surface-correction case changes a schematic response rule; it is not a laboratory
control that can be varied independently on the same real crystal while everything else stays fixed.

The tip's curvature/attachment explanation is verbally supported and visually highlighted, but
this milestone does not yet visualize a quantitative selection calculation. The faint parent insets,
shallow height correction and two-pass feedback deserve particular attention during complete
ordinary-speed rehearsal. A source observation or a properly identified existing model may improve
visual presence in final production, with the necessary provenance preserved.

whole-host play/pause/seek/Still and source-reader behavior, then review the complete temporary-voice
performance. Human listening, audience prediction/rephrasing, final voice timing, Mandarin semantic
