## E09 — inference and the reproducible example

The episode preserves the chain from observed face displacement/time to growth speed, then
transport-dependent local supersaturation and effective surface response. It does not present
surface humidity as directly measured or effective response as a literal single-collision tally.
The fitted barrier is not an abrupt threshold; the prefactor is not invariably an independently
measured quantity. Local anchors:
[response](../education/chapters/11-the-stickiness-of-ice.html#sticking),
[steps](../education/chapters/11-the-stickiness-of-ice.html#starting-a-layer),
[barrier](../education/chapters/11-the-stickiness-of-ice.html#the-barrier),
[inference](../education/chapters/11-the-stickiness-of-ice.html#where-the-numbers-come-from).

The source-bound example is monograph Fig. 4.4, printed p. 144: a prism face at −15 °C in
20 mbar background air, with the printed fit `alpha = exp(−3% / sigma_surface)` (implicit unit
prefactor). Provenance is also retained in `docs/libbrecht-parameters.md` and the chapter's
figure caption. For the episode's **chosen local** inputs:

```text
sigma_surface = 1%: alpha = exp(−3)   = 0.0497870683679
sigma_surface = 2%: alpha = exp(−1.5) = 0.223130160148
fixed-temperature speed ratio = (0.02 × exp(−1.5)) / (0.01 × exp(−3))
                              = 2 × exp(1.5) = 8.96337814068
```

These are calculations from a published fit, not new measured points, a reconstructed raw
dataset, or the effect of doubling a chamber setting. The draft states that status, defines the
one-percent ice-relative surplus, and uses “the fit gives” for the forward calculation.

The full accessible [Libbrecht–Rickerby primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.5982), especially
pp. 5–8 and Figures 4–6, supports the distinction between measured growth, transport correction
and fitted intrinsic response. Its selected small-crystal/low-pressure procedures do not define
a universal sample average. The reader now records that barrier and prefactor can be coupled,
and some analyses constrain the prefactor. This review did not re-digitize figures or re-fit
observations.

The accessible [2016 pressure study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.08528) qualifies the earlier
pressure-independence assumption: its model-based analysis finds substantially altered prism
kinetics near −5 °C as background pressure changes. The reader retains this correction. The
ending's low-pressure plates versus columns in air therefore remains a question involving
pressure, transport, seed and geometry, not a proof isolating facet width or establishing SDAK.
Local anchors:
[air influence](../education/chapters/05-the-restless-surface.html#when-the-air-is-not-inert),
[subunit prefactors](../education/chapters/11-the-stickiness-of-ice.html#a-less-than-one).
Premelting and impurity effects remain candidate interpretations, not established microscopic
explanations of every fitted parameter.

## E09 — independent lineage and source currency

**Levitation.** Harrison 2016 and the heterogeneous-record reanalysis in Pokrifka 2020 are one
observational lineage, not two replications. Under the apparatus assumptions, normalized voltage
supplies mass ratio; it does not image particle shape, grain count, orientation or facet speed.
Fitted changing responses and transformed mass-growth exponents do not directly observe a
molecular mechanism. Main locators are
[the moving coefficient](../education/chapters/11-the-stickiness-of-ice.html#the-coefficient-that-moves)
and `research/pokrifka-2020.md` anchors `P8X-POKRIFKA20-CUR-01`, `PROT-07`, `PROT-10`, `OBS-03`,
`INT-02` and `INT-03` (each short suffix carries the same `P8X-POKRIFKA20-` prefix).
Primary identities:
[Harrison 2016](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-15-0234.1),
[Pokrifka 2020](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-19-0303.1).

Access limit: this review relied on the existing accepted-manuscript extraction in
`research/pokrifka-2020.md`, which records its 56-page source and earlier text/figure inspection.
The [NSF manuscript record](https://par.nsf.gov/biblio/10164099) and direct publisher/full-text
retrieval did not yield an accessible fresh complete PDF in this review. The prior extraction's
page inspection and raw-data checks were not re-executed or relabelled as this review's work.

The [2024 morphology analysis](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-23-0131.1) concerns the newer, colder
[2023 dataset](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-22-0077.1). Indexed primary text was available while
direct publisher opening was restricted. The draft correctly limits the polycrystal caution to
that context; it does not retroactively establish grain counts in the older particles.

**Distinct theoretical description.** The Nelson–Baker composite framework is not automatically
the exponential broad-facet law; its characteristic supersaturation is not silently equated with
the latter's nucleation parameter. Primary references:
[Nelson–Baker 1996](https://doi.org/10.1029/95JD03162),
[author PDF](https://www.redmondphysicalsciences.com/nelson-baker1996TheoFram.pdf), and
[Wood, Baker and Calhoun 2001](https://doi.org/10.1029/2000JD900338).
The Wood comparison was limited to primary metadata/abstract and the existing source lineage;
no new full-text numerical extraction is claimed.

**2026 publication.** The current title is *Revisiting Theories for the Growth of Solid and Hollow
Single Crystals: The Importance of Step-Source Location*, Harrington and Pokrifka, JAS 83(7),
1175–1190, DOI `10.1175/JAS-D-26-0016.1`, published online 2 July 2026.
[Penn State primary metadata/abstract](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/revisiting-theories-for-the-growth-of-solid-and-hollow-single-cry/)
and the [publisher record](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/atsc/aop/JAS-D-26-0016.1/JAS-D-26-0016.1.xml)
were checked. The accessible abstract supports the limited discussion of step-source location,
comparison with growth observations and sensitivity to narrow hollow rims. Full text was not
accessible; equations, numerical fits and complete figures were not reviewed. No detailed
full-paper conclusion should be attributed to this review.

The [companion archive](https://www.datacommons.psu.edu/commonswizard/MetadataDisplay.aspx?Dataset=6511)
retains stale submitted-manuscript metadata. It does not supersede the published title, and its
code/shape functions are not new observations. The current local lineage index distinguishes

`#P8X-HP26-CUR-02`, `#P8X-HP26-CUR-03`, `#P8X-HP26-INT-01`, `#P8X-HP26-INT-02`.
Archive parsing and measurement reanalysis were not repeated here. The targeted currency check
