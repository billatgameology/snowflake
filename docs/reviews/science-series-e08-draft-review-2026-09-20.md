- Two temperatures establish a model-based surrounding reference; they do not directly measure
  vapour at a growing surface. This follows the original monograph's absent/small-test-crystal
  qualification, avoiding the stronger wording in the education summary.

  gap, ten-micrometre **radius**, limiting uptake assumption. Its denominator is 21. The main
  prototype uses qualitative comparisons, not that factor attached to arbitrary particles.
- Shortening the gap does not by itself eliminate other-crystal competition; liquid-balance
  verification is a separate setup without the test ice and uses a different reference from ice.
- Laser cycles measure thickness change, not the unknown starting thickness. Direction comes
  from the controlled sequence/other observations; oscillations alone do not establish growth
  rather than loss. The lower interface remains fixed in the drawn optical geometry.
- White-light absolute thickness and ideal speed sensitivity are not conflated with laser
  counting, nor with universal nanometre thickness precision.
- Levitation's voltage/mass relationship carries constant-charge, fixed-position and
  fixed-geometry assumptions. The depicted particle is explicitly a symbol with unmeasured
  shape. The source indexes qualify grain structure and later likely-polycrystalline lineage;
  the draft does not import the chapter's pristine-prism assumption.
- The 2016 traces and their corrected 2020 reanalysis are one observational lineage. E08 does
  not reuse superseded fitted kinetic values or claim independent replication between them.
