## Caption

Most of the measurements this channel runs on were funded by one man's credit card. Not a sad story — a map of where the open questions are, and how small the tools have to be.

## Production notes

**Exists:** renders; the 701-frame timeline (used at low contrast as a bed under the open-questions list); chart capability for the error-bar card.

**Must be built:** four line-drawn tool icons (chamber, microscope, clock, error bars); the grain-of-salt scale comparison; the schematic globe. All cheap.

**Difficulty:** low. A typography-and-voice piece.

**Tone guard — read the take back against two failure modes before locking:**
1. **Pity.** The 0:05 and 0:13 rows must be delivered flat and factual — budget facts, not a lament. No minor-key music under them.
2. **Swipe at funding agencies.** The line is "agencies fund work that promises something," which describes how funding works, not an accusation. Do not add a beat implying anyone was wrong to decline.

**Mandatory:** the 0:50 technical-difficulty row is not cuttable for time. Without it the video reads as "scientists don't know anything," which is the opposite of the source. If long, cut instead: "a day job" from 0:13, and shorten "much experimental evidence, little understanding of any of it" to "little understanding of any of it."

**Framing guard:** the researcher is never named in voiceover; attribution lives in the on-screen citation type. The subject is the size of the field, not the person.

**Rights:** no photographs of any kind. The globe is explicitly labelled schematic so it cannot be read as a claim about where researchers are.
