**Scope guard:** the opening claim is scoped to *natural* crystals — snow that falls is collected finished. Laboratory crystals **are** filmed growing (ch 4 cites a time-lapse; ch 6 Fig. 3.9 shows dendrites grown on a wire). Never say "nobody has ever filmed a snowflake growing" — it is false.
