## Caption

Wilson Bentley never faked a photograph. His album still misleads. 5,000 honest plates, ~100 specimens a season, only the photogenic ones kept. Automated cameras removed the choosing.

**RIGHTS — read before sourcing a single frame:**
- Bentley crystal plates: public domain, permitted, attribution strap on **every** frame.
- **The Bentley-with-his-rig photograph must NOT be pulled from a scan of the 1931 Bentley & Humphreys book without documented US public-domain status.** US published works are clear through 1930; 1931 works are not automatically clear until 1 Jan 2027. This is why the 8.0 s beat is a diagram, not that photograph. Do not swap without a written rights sign-off.
- Irregular-clump footage: USDA LT-SEM only. **The chapter's own irregular-crystal figure (Fig. 10.4) is Libbrecht's and is permanently off-limits.** If no public-domain LT-SEM frame can be confirmed, replace with our own 2D animation and re-tag DIAGRAM.

**Tone gate before publish:** one reviewer who has not read this brief watches the cut and answers one question — *"is this video accusing Bentley of anything?"* If yes, recut. The 31.5 s and 45.0 s lines carry the load.
