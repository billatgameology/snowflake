## Caption

The middle of a big snowflake quit growing early — starved by its own outer edge. The dry halo re-forms in about 50 ms while the ice takes minutes. The centre is very nearly the early crystal.

## Production notes

**Exists and does most of the work:** the 701-frame replay is the 20.0 s and 43.5 s beats and needs only a pinned-circle overlay and a speed ramp. The cell-true view is the closing shot as-is. The vapour-field animation covers 5.5 s.

**Must be built:** pinned-centre overlay plus a centre-only slow-motion pass (~half a day, compositing not simulation); a single-molecule random-walk trace (must stagger convincingly — a smooth inward curve would misrepresent diffusion); **the amount-versus-flow contour cutaway — the highest-care asset here.** It is what stops the video claiming tips sit in the richest air, which the source explicitly denies for thin plates. One day, checked against the chapter's Fig. 3.29 discussion before animation begins.

**Difficulty:** lowest of the render-heavy scripts, because the replay already exists.

**Number discipline:** the fifty-millisecond figure is only permitted on screen alongside its stated size and diffusion constant. **If a shorter cut drops the 35.0 s readout card, the fifty-millisecond claim comes out with it.**

**Hedge gate:** the 28.0 s line is not a candidate for trimming under any circumstance. Without it the video asserts the interior stops, which the source does not say.
