## Caption

"No two snowflakes are alike" has three answers depending on what alike means — and two of them are yes. The slogan only survives with the word "complex" in it.

## Title options

1. **No Two Alike — Three Answers, Two Of Them Yes** ← *use this one.* Contrarian, specific, and "two of them yes" is a real surprise that can't be resolved without watching
2. The Word That Makes It True — elegant and cryptic; good for an audience you already have, weak on a cold start
3. Two Snowflakes That Matched — slightly overpromises, since the source photo can't be shown

## Production notes

**Exists:** hero renders in fernlike and plain-plate looks; the twins beat can be two runs of the existing growth replay driven by one shared schedule — which is exactly what the claim describes.

**Must be built:** the isotope scatter grid (~6 s, easy); the feature-pointer pass over the fern crystal (~8 s, moderate — **placement must be hand-authored, not generated**, so nobody can read it as a measurement).

**CUT IN REVIEW — the books-on-a-shelf factorial beat.** It was twelve seconds that built a number (10¹⁵⁸) and then spent a mandatory held card telling the viewer the number doesn't mean what they just concluded. Self-negating, expensive to animate, and hard to follow once. The claim it supported — "about a hundred features, every one could have gone otherwise" — is fully delivered at 0:28 without it. **Removing it makes the video more honest, not less**, and returns twelve seconds to the strongest script on the slate.

**Copyright discipline:** the two source photographs this script leans on (the nearly-identical pair, Fig. 1.28; the twins, Fig. 1.29) are in copyright and are **not shown**. Our renders illustrate the described situation, carry MODEL tags, and carry an on-screen card saying the source photo is not shown. Do not let a MODEL render read as the published photograph.

**If the factorial is ever restored:** the 10¹⁵⁸ figure is a books-on-a-shelf **analogy that Libbrecht explicitly flags as one** ("the math ends up being similar"; "the exact calculation would depend on the details"). It must never be stated as a count of possible snowflakes, and the analogy card must stay legible for the whole beat. This is the single easiest way for this channel to commit exactly the error it exists to avoid.

**Twins beat integrity:** the two runs must come from genuinely identical schedules with a genuinely different seed. If both runs are bit-identical it is a tautology, not a twin.

**Difficulty:** medium. Mostly reuse plus three modest 2D builds.
