## Caption

The flat faces on a snowflake are the losers. Rough surfaces catch almost everything, race outward, and grow themselves out of existence. The hexagon is just what's left.

## Production notes

**Exists:** finished-crystal renders in multiple looks; timeline scrub for the facet-advance beat.

**Must be built:** the whole 2D spine — even-arrow rain, rough-vs-smooth capture pair, the eat-itself time-lapse, the quartz/copper pair, the exaggerated concave cutaway. Five original 2D animations; that is the bulk of the job.

**Difficulty:** medium–high, almost entirely 2D. No new solver runs.

**Hard note for the animator:** the eat-itself time-lapse must never show a flat face stationary. It advances; it is simply the slowest thing on screen. Ghost trails sell "grew itself out of existence" without implying facets are frozen.

**Hard note:** the concave cutaway carries its exaggeration label for its entire duration, not as a flash. The real dish is a few molecular layers.
