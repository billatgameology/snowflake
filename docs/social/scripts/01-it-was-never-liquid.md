## Caption

Only one part of a snow crystal was ever a drop of water. It's about one hundred-thousandth of the finished thing.

*(Note: the corpus separates "snowflake" from "snow crystal" — a puffball is hundreds of crystals with no symmetry to explain. Voiceover uses "snow crystal" for the 100,000-droplet budget, following the chapter's own correction. The looser word is acceptable in a caption for reach.)*
