# Open questions and things to check

State of the exploration as of 2026-08-04. Nothing here is decided unless it says so.

## Must be checked before it is spoken

Two claims entered the conversation from general knowledge rather than from the corpus. Both
are plausible and neither is in chapters 1–13. **Do not put them in a voiceover until
sourced.**

| Claim | Status | What the corpus actually says |
|---|---|---|
| Nakaya grew his first crystal **on a rabbit hair** | **UNSOURCED** | Ch. 2 and 10 say "a thin fibre suspended in a chamber." The rabbit-hair detail is widely repeated and may well be right — it needs a primary source (Nakaya 1954) before it is spoken. |
| Kepler wrote *The Six-Cornered Snowflake* because he **couldn't afford a present** | **UNSOURCED** | Ch. 2 says only that he presented it to his patron, Emperor Rudolf II, as a New Year's Day gift. The financial framing is an addition. |

General rule this establishes: **anything colourful that isn't in the chapters gets sourced
before it is said.** The chapters themselves are scrupulous about this — they even flag a
published caption's own date error (Bentley's "1865" for 1885) rather than silently fixing
it.

## Decided

- Voiceover by the maker.
- Young audience, hooky format, Mark Rober style — education smuggled inside entertainment.
- Hooks first, pattern-driven, **not** a strict chapter sequence.
- Scope is chapters 1–13.
- Build whatever the project needs to serve science, the education site, and social together.
- **(2026-08-05)** The promise, seven formats, publishing order, cadence and failure-mode
  guardrails are settled in [series-bible.md](series-bible.md). Twelve scripts written,
  fact-checked and editorially reviewed.

## Decided against

- **The build as the throughline from piece one.** Considered and rejected for Series 1: it
  ties the channel's ceiling to work that is incomplete and out of chapter 1–13 scope, and
  it makes the creator the subject at a moment when breadth is the strategy. **Held as the
  Series 2 promise**, where chapters 14–29 make it true.
- **Two titles**, killed as actively dangerous — see [review-findings.md](review-findings.md).

## Animation that does not exist yet

Everything generated on the sibling exploration branch is *G-G crystal renders* — the
spectacle. The teaching needs explanatory animation nobody has built:

- one bent water molecule → tetrahedra → the honeycomb (ch. 3)
- the loop-closing argument: three, four, five failing and six succeeding (ch. 3)
- the vapour field draining around a growing crystal — **the app already visualises this**
  (Phase 3 vapour slice) and it is the most under-used asset in the repository (ch. 4)
- the runaway bump: flat face, tiny corner, corner wins (ch. 6)
- the Nakaya diagram as a living map — temperature slider, habit flipping four times (ch. 7)
- faceted vs rounded at molecular scale; the 50-nanometre sliver at the rim (ch. 12)
- **the four kinds of line** — the recurring confidence graphic (ch. 13)

Note that chapters 1–13 already ship a large number of original interactives and animations
(ch. 3 alone lists six). Those are a starting point, not a blocker — the question is what
transfers to video and what has to be rebuilt.
