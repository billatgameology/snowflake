Why the images are not here

These 139 figures are copyrighted third-party research media. This site cites them but does not republish them, so on the published site each one appears as a card naming the paper, figure and page rather than as an image. The site-authored explanations, diagrams and animations remain available without those bytes.

To see locally cached source media inline, create the private personal edition with node docs/education/tools/build-local.mjs. The authored pages do not probe research/, even inside a checkout. The canonical record is FIGURES.md, which also carries each file’s size and SHA-256 so a local copy can be verified byte for byte.
