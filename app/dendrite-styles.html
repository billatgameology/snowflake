RECORDED MODEL GROWTH · FOUR ARTISTIC WAYS TO SEE IT

What am I seeing?

Each site comes from recorded Gravner–Griffeath or G-G+ model growth and appears no earlier than its attachment tick. Composed views arrange independently grown crystals using the catalogue's recorded transforms and phase offsets; they are not one simulated crystal. Sites are not molecules. Ion Bloom and Timeglass use artistic light and colour. Three Views shows the same recording from above, at a low angle and in a magnified branch detail. The marker identifies the detail center; each pane can be turned independently by dragging. Recorded height is preserved. Crystal Cast reconstructs a relief from the projected sites, adding artistic bevels and shadows; it is not simulated ice optics. Recorded site coordinates and explicit scene transforms are preserved at unit lattice Z scale. Playback seconds are presentation time, not physical growth time. This is model output, not validated laboratory growth.

Focus a study to drag its view. Double-click to reset. Space pauses; R replays; Escape returns to comparison. The same playhead drives all four studies.
