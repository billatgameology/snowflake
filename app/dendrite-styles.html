What am I seeing?

Each point is a recorded lattice site from a Gravner–Griffeath or G-G+ model run, revealed at its attachment tick. Composed views arrange independently grown crystals using the catalogue's recorded transforms and phase offsets; they are not one simulated crystal. Points are not molecules. Light and colour are artistic encodings; Chronograph also displaces sites in depth according to their arrival time. Other views keep recorded sites and any explicit scene transforms, at unit lattice Z scale. Playback seconds are presentation time, not physical growth time. This is model output, not validated laboratory growth.

Focus a study to drag its view. Double-click to reset. Space pauses; R replays; Escape returns to comparison. The same playhead drives all four studies.
