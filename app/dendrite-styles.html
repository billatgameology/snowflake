What am I seeing?

Each point is a recorded lattice site from one Gravner–Griffeath model run, revealed at its attachment tick. Points are not molecules. Light and colour are artistic encodings; Chronograph also displaces sites in depth according to their arrival time. The other views keep the recorded coordinates. Playback seconds are presentation time, not physical growth time. This is model output, not validated laboratory growth.

Focus a study to drag its view. Double-click to reset. Space pauses; R replays; Escape returns to comparison. The same playhead drives all four studies.
